# USENIX Security 2024

This list focuses on accepted papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems. Papers were screened by title from the [USENIX Security 2024 official paper list or proceedings](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/technical-sessions) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each `Research question` and `What they did` line is one sentence, and every method summary is grounded in the paper abstract.

## 1. LLM and Agent Security

- [Fuzzing BusyBox: Leveraging LLM and Crash Reuse for Embedded Bug Unearthing](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/asmita)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Fuzzing BusyBox: Leveraging LLM and Crash Reuse for Embedded Bug Unearthing” investigate?
  - **What they did:** Within this study, the authors introduce two techniques to fortify software testing.
- [Formalizing and Benchmarking Prompt Injection Attacks and Defenses](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/liu-yupei)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Formalizing and Benchmarking Prompt Injection Attacks and Defenses” investigate?
  - **What they did:** Moreover, based on their framework, the authors design a new attack by combining existing ones.
- [Don't Listen To Me: Understanding and Exploring Jailbreak Prompts of Large Language Models](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/yu-zhiyuan)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Don't Listen To Me: Understanding and Exploring Jailbreak Prompts of Large Language Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** Further, the authors conducted a user study involving 92 participants with diverse backgrounds to unveil the process of manually creating jailbreak prompts.
- [Malla: Demystifying Real-world Large Language Model Integrated Malicious Services](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/lin-zilong)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Malla: Demystifying Real-world Large Language Model Integrated Malicious Services” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors conduct the first systematic study on 212 real-world Mallas, uncovering their proliferation in underground marketplaces and exposing their operational modalities.
- [Making Them Ask and Answer: Jailbreaking Large Language Models in Few Queries via Disguise and Reconstruction](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/liu-tong)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Making Them Ask and Answer: Jailbreaking Large Language Models in Few Queries via Disguise and Reconstruction” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors evaluate DRA across various open-source and closed-source models, showcasing state-of-the-art jailbreak success rates and attack efficiency.
- [From One Thousand Pages of Specification to Unveiling Hidden Bugs: Large Language Model Assisted Fuzzing of Matter IoT Devices](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/ma-xiaoyue)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “From One Thousand Pages of Specification to Unveiling Hidden Bugs: Large Language Model Assisted Fuzzing of Matter IoT Devices” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To overcome this challenge, the authors leverage a large language model to successfully automate the conversion process. mGPTFuzz conducts stateful analysis, which generates message sequences to uncover bugs that would be challenging to discover otherwise.
- [Moderating Illicit Online Image Promotion for Unsafe User Generated Content Games Using Large Vision-Language Models](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/guo-keyan)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Moderating Illicit Online Image Promotion for Unsafe User Generated Content Games Using Large Vision-Language Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors collect a real-world dataset comprising 2,924 images that display diverse sexually explicit and violent content used to promote UGCGs by their game creators.

## 2. Backdoors, Poisoning, and Adversarial ML

- [Improving the Ability of Thermal Radiation Based Hardware Trojan Detection](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/su-ting)
  - **Research question:** How does “Improving the Ability of Thermal Radiation Based Hardware Trojan Detection” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To this end, this paper introduces a countermeasure named noise based pixel occupation enhancement (NICE), aiming to improve the ability of TR-based HT detection.
- [Lotto: Secure Participant Selection against Adversarial Servers in Federated Learning](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/jiang-zhifeng)
  - **Research question:** How does “Lotto: Secure Participant Selection against Adversarial Servers in Federated Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors present Lotto, an FL system that addresses this fundamental, yet underexplored issue by providing secure participant selection against an adversarial server.
- [Tossing in the Dark: Practical Bit-Flipping on Gray-box Deep Neural Networks for Runtime Trojan Injection](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/wang-zihao-tossing)
  - **Research question:** How does “Tossing in the Dark: Practical Bit-Flipping on Gray-box Deep Neural Networks for Runtime Trojan Injection” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To address the unique challenge posed by the black-box decoder to Trojan injection in this scenario, the authors developed a suite of innovative techniques.
- [An LLM-Assisted Easy-to-Trigger Backdoor Attack on Code Completion Models: Injecting Disguised Vulnerabilities against Strong Detection](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/yan)
  - **Research question:** How does “An LLM-Assisted Easy-to-Trigger Backdoor Attack on Code Completion Models: Injecting Disguised Vulnerabilities against Strong Detection” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?

  - **Research question:** How does “Instruction Backdoor Attacks Against Customized LLMs” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?

- [Neural Network Semantic Backdoor Detection and Mitigation: A Causality-Based Approach](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/sun-bing)

- [On the Difficulty of Defending Contrastive Learning against Backdoor Attacks](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/li-changjiang)
  - **Research question:** How does “On the Difficulty of Defending Contrastive Learning against Backdoor Attacks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?

- [SecurityNet: Assessing Machine Learning Vulnerabilities on Public Models](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/zhang-boyang)
  - **Research question:** How does “SecurityNet: Assessing Machine Learning Vulnerabilities on Public Models” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?

  - **Research question:** How does “ACE: A Model Poisoning Attack on Contribution Evaluation Methods in Federated Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?

## 3. AI Privacy, Memorization, and Unlearning

- [FAMOS: Robust Privacy-Preserving Authentication on Payment Apps via Federated Multi-Modal Contrastive Learning](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/cai-yifeng)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “FAMOS: Robust Privacy-Preserving Authentication on Payment Apps via Federated Multi-Modal Contrastive Learning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce FAMOS, a novel authentication framework based on federated multi-modal contrastive learning.
- [Efficient Privacy Auditing in Federated Learning](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/chang)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Efficient Privacy Auditing in Federated Learning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors design a novel efficient membership inference attack to audit privacy risks in federated learning.
- [Defending Against Data Reconstruction Attacks in Federated Learning: An Information Theory Approach](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/tan)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Defending Against Data Reconstruction Attacks in Federated Learning: An Information Theory Approach” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To quantify the information leakage incurred by FL, the authors establish a channel model, which depends on the upper bound of joint mutual information between the local dataset and multiple transmitted parameters.
- [DPAdapter: Improving Differentially Private Deep Learning through Noise Tolerance Pre-training](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/wang-zihao-dpadapter)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “DPAdapter: Improving Differentially Private Deep Learning through Noise Tolerance Pre-training” investigate?
  - **What they did:** In response, the authors introduce DPAdapter, a pioneering technique designed to amplify the model performance of DPML algorithms by enhancing parameter robustness.
- [Holding Secrets Accountable: Auditing Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/lycklama)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Holding Secrets Accountable: Auditing Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors evaluate the performance of their framework when instantiated with their consistency protocol and compare it to hashing-based and homomorphic-commitment-based approaches, demonstrating that it is up to 10^4× faster and up to 10^6× more concise.
- [Fast and Private Inference of Deep Neural Networks by Co-designing Activation Functions](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/diaa)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Fast and Private Inference of Deep Neural Networks by Co-designing Activation Functions” investigate?
  - **What they did:** Furthermore, to address the accuracy issues previously encountered with polynomial activations, the authors propose a novel training algorithm that gives accuracy competitive with plaintext models.
- [MD-ML: Super Fast Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning for Malicious Security with a Dishonest Majority](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/yuan)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “MD-ML: Super Fast Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning for Malicious Security with a Dishonest Majority” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose MD-ML, a framework for Maliciously secure Dishonest majority PPML, with a focus on boosting online efficiency.
- [A Linear Reconstruction Approach for Attribute Inference Attacks against Synthetic Data](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/annamalai-linear)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “A Linear Reconstruction Approach for Attribute Inference Attacks against Synthetic Data” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors evaluate their attack on state-of-the-art SDG algorithms, including Probabilistic Graphical Models, Generative Adversarial Networks, and recent differentially private SDG mechanisms.
- [Did the Neurons Read your Book? Document-level Membership Inference for Large Language Models](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/meeus)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Did the Neurons Read your Book? Document-level Membership Inference for Large Language Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors show their methodology to perform very well, reaching an AUC of 0.856 for books and 0.678 for papers.
- [MIST: Defending Against Membership Inference Attacks Through Membership-Invariant Subspace Training](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/li-jiacheng)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “MIST: Defending Against Membership Inference Attacks Through Membership-Invariant Subspace Training” investigate?
  - **What they did:** Leveraging two distinct recent advancements in representation learning: counterfactually-invariant representations and subspace learning methods, the authors introduce a novel Membership-Invariant Subspace Training (MIST) method to defend against MI attacks.
- [Inf2Guard: An Information-Theoretic Framework for Learning Privacy-Preserving Representations against Inference Attacks](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/noorbakhsh)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Inf2Guard: An Information-Theoretic Framework for Learning Privacy-Preserving Representations against Inference Attacks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors address these limitations by proposing an information-theoretic defense framework, called Inf2Guard, against the three major types of inference attacks.
- [SoK: Neural Network Extraction Through Physical Side Channels](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/horvath)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “SoK: Neural Network Extraction Through Physical Side Channels” investigate?
  - **What they did:** Specifically, the authors discuss the dependencies of threat models on attack objectives and analysis methods, for which the authors present a novel systematic attack framework composed of fundamental stages derived from various attacks.
- [UBA-Inf: Unlearning Activated Backdoor Attack with Influence-Driven Camouflage](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/huang-zirui)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “UBA-Inf: Unlearning Activated Backdoor Attack with Influence-Driven Camouflage” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors report a new threat against models with unlearning enabled and implement an Unlearning Activated Backdoor Attack with Influence-driven camouflage (UBA-Inf).
- [DAAP: Privacy-Preserving Model Accuracy Estimation on Unlabeled Datasets Through Distribution-Aware Adversarial Perturbation](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/cao-guodong)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “DAAP: Privacy-Preserving Model Accuracy Estimation on Unlabeled Datasets Through Distribution-Aware Adversarial Perturbation” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address these issues, the authors propose a novel approach: Distribution-Aware Adversarial Perturbation (DAAP).
- [PrivImage: Differentially Private Synthetic Image Generation using Diffusion Models with Semantic-Aware Pretraining](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/li-kecen)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “PrivImage: Differentially Private Synthetic Image Generation using Diffusion Models with Semantic-Aware Pretraining” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a novel DP image synthesis method, termed PRIVIMAGE, which meticulously selects pre-training data, promoting the efficient creation of DP datasets with high fidelity and utility.
- ["I Don't Know If We're Doing Good. I Don't Know If We're Doing Bad": Investigating How Practitioners Scope, Motivate, and Conduct Privacy Work When Developing AI Products](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/lee)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “"I Don't Know If We're Doing Good. I Don't Know If We're Doing Bad": Investigating How Practitioners Scope, Motivate, and Conduct Privacy Work When Developing AI Products” investigate?
  - **What they did:** Respecting privacy is a key principle for developing ethical, human-centered AI systems, but the authors cannot hope to better support practitioners without answers to that question.
- [SoK: All You Need to Know About On-Device ML Model Extraction - The Gap Between Research and Practice](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/nayan)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “SoK: All You Need to Know About On-Device ML Model Extraction - The Gap Between Research and Practice” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors evaluated well known research projects from existing work with real-world ML models, and discussed their reproducibility, computation complexity, and power consumption.
- [Unveiling the Secrets without Data: Can Graph Neural Networks Be Exploited through Data-Free Model Extraction Attacks?](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/zhuang)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Unveiling the Secrets without Data: Can Graph Neural Networks Be Exploited through Data-Free Model Extraction Attacks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address this issue, the authors propose StealGNN, the first data-free model extraction attack framework against GNNs.
- [ModelGuard: Information-Theoretic Defense Against Model Extraction Attacks](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/tang)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “ModelGuard: Information-Theoretic Defense Against Model Extraction Attacks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a novel prediction perturbation defense named ModelGuard, which aims at defending against adaptive model extraction attacks while maintaining a high utility of the protected system.
- [Quantifying Privacy Risks of Prompts in Visual Prompt Learning](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/wu-yixin)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Quantifying Privacy Risks of Prompts in Visual Prompt Learning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors also demonstrate that the adversary can mount a successful property inference attack with limited cost.
- [How Does a Deep Learning Model Architecture Impact Its Privacy? A Comprehensive Study of Privacy Attacks on CNNs and Transformers](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/zhang-guangsheng)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “How Does a Deep Learning Model Architecture Impact Its Privacy? A Comprehensive Study of Privacy Attacks on CNNs and Transformers” investigate?
  - **What they did:** By investigating representative model architectures from convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to Transformers, the authors demonstrate that Transformers generally exhibit higher vulnerability to privacy attacks than CNNs.
- [Privacy Side Channels in Machine Learning Systems](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/debenedetti)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Privacy Side Channels in Machine Learning Systems” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce privacy side channels: attacks that exploit these system-level components to extract private information at far higher rates than is otherwise possible for standalone models.
- [FaceObfuscator: Defending Deep Learning-based Privacy Attacks with Gradient Descent-resistant Features in Face Recognition](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/jin-shuaifan)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “FaceObfuscator: Defending Deep Learning-based Privacy Attacks with Gradient Descent-resistant Features in Face Recognition” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address this threat, the authors introduce FaceObfuscator, a lightweight privacy-preserving face recognition system that first removes visual information that is non-crucial for face recognition from facial images via frequency domain and then generates obfuscated features interleaved in the feature space to resist gradient descent in DL-based reconstruction attacks.

- [DeepEclipse: How to Break White-Box DNN-Watermarking Schemes](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/pegoraro)
  - **Research question:** How does “DeepEclipse: How to Break White-Box DNN-Watermarking Schemes” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose DeepEclipse, a novel and unified framework designed to remove white-box watermarks.
- [Deciphering Textual Authenticity: A Generalized Strategy through the Lens of Large Language Semantics for Detecting Human vs. Machine-Generated Text](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/bethany)
  - **Research question:** How does “Deciphering Textual Authenticity: A Generalized Strategy through the Lens of Large Language Semantics for Detecting Human vs. Machine-Generated Text” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?

- [False Claims against Model Ownership Resolution](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/liu-jian)
  - **Research question:** How does “False Claims against Model Ownership Resolution” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** The authors show that common MOR schemes in the literature are vulnerable to a different, equally important but insufficiently explored, robustness concern: a malicious accuser.

## 6. Other AI Security and Privacy
