# NDSS 2023

This list focuses on accepted papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems. Papers were screened by title from the [NDSS 2023 official paper list or proceedings](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/ndss2023/accepted-papers/) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each `Research question` and `What they did` line is one sentence, and every method summary is grounded in the paper abstract.

## 1. Backdoors, Poisoning, and Adversarial ML

- [Adversarial Robustness for Tabular Data through Cost and Utility Awareness](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/ndss-paper/adversarial-robustness-for-tabular-data-through-cost-and-utility-awareness/)
  - **Research question:** How does “Adversarial Robustness for Tabular Data through Cost and Utility Awareness” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors show that their approach is effective on three datasets corresponding to applications for which adversarial examples can have economic and social implications.
- [Backdoor Attacks Against Dataset Distillation](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/ndss-paper/backdoor-attacks-against-dataset-distillation/)
  - **Research question:** How does “Backdoor Attacks Against Dataset Distillation” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce the first backdoor attacks on dataset-distilled models, including NAIVEATTACK and an iterative trigger-updating method called DOORPING.
- [BARS: Local Robustness Certification for Deep Learning based Traffic Analysis Systems](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/ndss-paper/bars-local-robustness-certification-for-deep-learning-based-traffic-analysis-systems/)
  - **Research question:** How does “BARS: Local Robustness Certification for Deep Learning based Traffic Analysis Systems” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Therefore, the authors propose BARS, a general robustness certification framework for DL-based traffic analysis systems based on boundary-adaptive randomized smoothing.
- [BEAGLE: Forensics of Deep Learning Backdoor Attack for Better Defense](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/ndss-paper/beagle-forensics-of-deep-learning-backdoor-attack-for-better-defense/)
  - **Research question:** How does “BEAGLE: Forensics of Deep Learning Backdoor Attack for Better Defense” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a novel model backdoor forensics technique.
- [RoVISQ: Reduction of Video Service Quality via Adversarial Attacks on Deep Learning-based Video Compression](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/ndss-paper/rovisq-reduction-of-video-service-quality-via-adversarial-attacks-on-deep-learning-based-video-compression/)
  - **Research question:** How does “RoVISQ: Reduction of Video Service Quality via Adversarial Attacks on Deep Learning-based Video Compression” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors conduct the first systematic study for adversarial attacks on deep learning-based video compression and downstream classification systems.
- [Securing Federated Sensitive Topic Classification against Poisoning Attacks](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/ndss-paper/securing-federated-sensitive-topic-classification-against-poisoning-attacks/)
  - **Research question:** How does “Securing Federated Sensitive Topic Classification against Poisoning Attacks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors present a Federated Learning (FL) based solution for building a distributed classifier capable of detecting URLs containing sensitive content, i.e., content related to categories such as health, political beliefs, sexual orientation, etc.
- [The “Beatrix” Resurrections: Robust Backdoor Detection via Gram Matrices](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/ndss-paper/the-beatrix-resurrections-robust-backdoor-detection-via-gram-matrices/)
  - **Research question:** How does “The “Beatrix” Resurrections: Robust Backdoor Detection via Gram Matrices” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors demonstrate the effectiveness of their method through extensive evaluation and comparison with state-of-the-art defensive techniques.

## 2. AI Privacy, Memorization, and Unlearning

- [Machine Unlearning of Features and Labels](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/ndss-paper/machine-unlearning-of-features-and-labels/)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Machine Unlearning of Features and Labels” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose the first method for unlearning features and labels.

## 3. Secure and Verifiable AI Systems

- [Fusion: Efficient and Secure Inference Resilient to Malicious Servers](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/ndss-paper/fusion-efficient-and-secure-inference-resilient-to-malicious-servers/)
  - **Research question:** How does “Fusion: Efficient and Secure Inference Resilient to Malicious Servers” make AI training or inference more secure, private, or verifiable?
  - **What they did:** To address these issues, the authors propose textit{Fusion}, where the client mixes the public samples (which have known query results) with their own samples to be queried as the inputs of multi-party computation to jointly perform the secure inference.

## 4. AI for Cybersecurity and Abuse Detection

- [Sometimes, You Aren’t What You Do: Mimicry Attacks against Provenance Graph Host Intrusion Detection Systems](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/ndss-paper/sometimes-you-arent-what-you-do-mimicry-attacks-against-provenance-graph-host-intrusion-detection-systems/)
  - **Research question:** How does “Sometimes, You Aren’t What You Do: Mimicry Attacks against Provenance Graph Host Intrusion Detection Systems” use AI to improve a conventional security or abuse-detection task?
  - **What they did:** Against a corpus of exemplar Prov-HIDS, the authors develop evasion tactics that allow attackers to hide within benign process behaviors.

## 5. Other AI Security and Privacy

- [PPA: Preference Profiling Attack Against Federated Learning](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/ndss-paper/ppa-preference-profiling-attack-against-federated-learning/)
  - **Research question:** What AI security or privacy weakness does “PPA: Preference Profiling Attack Against Federated Learning” expose or mitigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a new type of privacy inference attack, coined Preference Profiling Attack (PPA), that accurately profiles the private preferences of a local user, e.g., most liked (disliked) items from the client's online shopping and most common expressions from the user's selfies.
