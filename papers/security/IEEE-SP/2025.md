# IEEE S&P 2025

This list focuses on accepted papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems. Papers were screened by title from the [IEEE S&P 2025 official paper list](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each `Research question` and `What they did` line is one sentence. Method summaries are grounded in the abstract; if no public abstract was available, the entry says so explicitly instead of inferring a contribution from the title.

## 1. LLM and Agent Security

- [Supporting Human Raters with the Detection of Harmful Content using Large Language Models](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-42)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Supporting Human Raters with the Detection of Harmful Content using Large Language Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** Using a dataset of 50,000 user comments, the authors demonstrate that LLMs can achieve 90 % accuracy when compared to human verdicts.
- [On the (In)Security of LLM App Stores](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-142)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “On the (In)Security of LLM App Stores” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a three-layer concern framework to identify the potential security risks of LLM apps, i.e., LLM apps with abusive potential, LLM apps with malicious intent, and LLM apps with backdoors.
- [DataSentinel: A Game-Theoretic Detection of Prompt Injection Attacks](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-152)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “DataSentinel: A Game-Theoretic Detection of Prompt Injection Attacks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose DataSentinel, a game-theoretic method to detect prompt injection attacks.
- [Make a Feint to the East While Attacking in the West: Blinding LLM-Based Code Auditors with Flashboom Attacks](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-191)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Make a Feint to the East While Attacking in the West: Blinding LLM-Based Code Auditors with Flashboom Attacks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To scale this approach, the authors present Crazy-Ivan11Source code, dataset and attack results are available at https://github.com/oxygen-hunter/Flashboom., an automated system that identifies and seamlessly integrates high-attention code snippets, shifting focus away from genuine vulnerabilities to decoy functions.
- [Fun-tuning: Characterizing the Vulnerability of Proprietary LLMs to Optimization-based Prompt Injection Attacks via the Fine-Tuning Interface](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-228)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Fun-tuning: Characterizing the Vulnerability of Proprietary LLMs to Optimization-based Prompt Injection Attacks via the Fine-Tuning Interface” investigate?
  - **What they did:** Specifically, the authors characterize how an attacker can leverage the loss-like information returned from the remote fine-tuning interface to guide the search for adversarial prompts.
- [Fuzz-Testing Meets LLM-Based Agents: An Automated and Efficient Framework for Jailbreaking Text-To-Image Generation Models](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-233)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Fuzz-Testing Meets LLM-Based Agents: An Automated and Efficient Framework for Jailbreaking Text-To-Image Generation Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose JailFuzzer, a novel fuzzing framework driven by large language model (LLM) agents, designed to efficiently generate natural and semantically meaningful jailbreak prompts in a black-box setting.
- [Modifier Unlocked: Jailbreaking Text-to-Image Models Through Prompts](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-248)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Modifier Unlocked: Jailbreaking Text-to-Image Models Through Prompts” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose MODX, the first modifier-based attack framework for jailbreaking text-to-image models.

## 2. Backdoors, Poisoning, and Adversarial ML

- [BAIT: Large Language Model Backdoor Scanning by Inverting Attack Target](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-94)
  - **Research question:** How does “BAIT: Large Language Model Backdoor Scanning by Inverting Attack Target” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors conduct a theoretical analysis of the LLM backdoor learning process under specific assumptions, revealing that the autoregressive training paradigm in causal language models inherently induces strong causal relationships among tokens in backdoor targets.
- [PEFTGuard: Detecting Backdoor Attacks Against Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-160)
  - **Research question:** How does “PEFTGuard: Detecting Backdoor Attacks Against Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To fill this gap, the authors first construct and release PADBench, a comprehensive benchmark that contains 13, 300 benign and backdoored adapters fine-tuned with various datasets, attack strategies, PEFT methods, and LLMs.
- [On the Conflict between Robustness and Learning in Collaborative Machine Learning](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-168)
  - **Research question:** How does “On the Conflict between Robustness and Learning in Collaborative Machine Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors empirically demonstrate the correctness of their theoretical findings on a selection of existing robust aggregators and relevant applications, including end-to-end results where the authors show that using existing robust aggregators can lead to an adversary can…
- [Secure Transfer Learning: Training Clean Model Against Backdoor in Pre-Trained Encoder and Downstream Dataset](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-185)
  - **Research question:** How does “Secure Transfer Learning: Training Clean Model Against Backdoor in Pre-Trained Encoder and Downstream Dataset” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors investigate how to mitigate potential backdoor risks in resource-constrained transfer learning scenarios.
- [Not All Edges are Equally Robust: Evaluating the Robustness of Ranking-Based Federated Learning](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-210)
  - **Research question:** How does “Not All Edges are Equally Robust: Evaluating the Robustness of Ranking-Based Federated Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Through extensive experiments on benchmark datasets, the authors demonstrate that their attack achieves an overall 53.23 % attack impact and is 3.7× more impactful than existing methods.
- [Practical Poisoning Attacks with Limited Byzantine Clients in Clustered Federated Learning](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-237)
  - **Research question:** How does “Practical Poisoning Attacks with Limited Byzantine Clients in Clustered Federated Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors present novel powerful CFL-specific poisoning attacks, named Cluster-U-M and Cluster-U-D.

## 3. AI Privacy, Memorization, and Unlearning

- [Edge Unlearning is Not "on Edge"! An Adaptive Exact Unlearning System on Resource-Constrained Devices](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-13)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Edge Unlearning is Not "on Edge"! An Adaptive Exact Unlearning System on Resource-Constrained Devices” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a Constraint-aware Adaptive Exact Unlearning System at the network Edge (CAUSE), an approach to enabling exact unlearning on resource-constrained devices.
- [Prevalence Overshadows Concerns? Understanding Chinese Users' Privacy Awareness and Expectations Towards LLM-based Healthcare Consultation](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-23)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Prevalence Overshadows Concerns? Understanding Chinese Users' Privacy Awareness and Expectations Towards LLM-based Healthcare Consultation” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To fill this gap, this paper contributes the first user study (n=846) in China on privacy awareness and expectations in LLM-based healthcare consultations.
- [Comet: Accelerating Private Inference for Large Language Model by Predicting Activation Sparsity](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-115)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Comet: Accelerating Private Inference for Large Language Model by Predicting Activation Sparsity” investigate?
  - **What they did:** Inspired by the prevalent activation sparsity of LLMs, where most neuron are not activated after non-linear activation functions, the authors propose an efficient private inference system, Comet.
- [Rigging the Foundation: Manipulating Pre-training for Advanced Membership Inference Attacks](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-182)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Rigging the Foundation: Manipulating Pre-training for Advanced Membership Inference Attacks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address this challenge, the authors introduce a new technique - active robustness overfitting (ARO).
- [Machine Learning with Privacy for Protected Attributes](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-229)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Machine Learning with Privacy for Protected Attributes” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors also propose a modification of the standard DP-SGD algorithm that satisfies FDP while leveraging desirable properties such as amplification via sub-sampling.
- [Is MPC Secure? Leveraging Neural Network Classifiers to Detect Data Leakage Vulnerabilities in MPC Implementations](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-235)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Is MPC Secure? Leveraging Neural Network Classifiers to Detect Data Leakage Vulnerabilities in MPC Implementations” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose MPCGuard, a practical frame-work for detecting data leakage vulnerabilities in MPC imple-mentations.
- [Understanding Users' Security and Privacy Concerns and Attitudes Towards Conversational AI Platforms](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-247)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Understanding Users' Security and Privacy Concerns and Attitudes Towards Conversational AI Platforms” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors conduct a large-scale analysis of over 2.5M user posts from the r/ChatGPT Reddit community to understand users' security and privacy concerns and attitudes toward conversational AI platforms.

## 4. Secure and Verifiable AI Systems

- [Shark: Actively Secure Inference using Function Secret Sharing](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-145)
  - **Research question:** How does “Shark: Actively Secure Inference using Function Secret Sharing” make AI training or inference more secure, private, or verifiable?
  - **What they did:** To demonstrate the effectiveness of their approach the authors build SHARK-the first FSS-based system for actively secure inference-which outperforms the state-of-the-art by up to 2300×.

## 5. Deepfakes, Watermarking, and Content Provenance

- [Watermarking Language Models for Many Adaptive Users](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-45)
  - **Research question:** How does “Watermarking Language Models for Many Adaptive Users” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** The authors study watermarking schemes for language models with provable guarantees.
- [UnMarker: A Universal Attack on Defensive Image Watermarking](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-72)
  - **Research question:** How does “UnMarker: A Universal Attack on Defensive Image Watermarking” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** The authors present UnMarker— the first practical universal attack on defensive watermarking.
- [SoK: Dataset Copyright Auditing in Machine Learning Systems](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-101)
  - **Research question:** How does “SoK: Dataset Copyright Auditing in Machine Learning Systems” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** Concretely, the authors categorize dataset copyright auditing research into two prominent strands: intrusive methods and non-intrusive methods, depending on whether they require modifications to the original dataset.
- [SoK: Watermarking for AI-Generated Content](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-240)
  - **Research question:** How does “SoK: Watermarking for AI-Generated Content” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** The authors formalize the definitions and desired properties of watermarking schemes and examine the key objectives and threat models for existing approaches.

## 6. AI Safety, Alignment, and Responsible AI

- [Exploring Parent-Child Perspectives on Safety in Generative AI: Concerns, Mitigation Strategies, and Design Implications](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-37)
  - **Research question:** How does “Exploring Parent-Child Perspectives on Safety in Generative AI: Concerns, Mitigation Strategies, and Design Implications” evaluate or improve AI safety, alignment, oversight, or responsible deployment?
  - **What they did:** To identify risks and understand parental controls challenges, the authors conducted a content analysis on Reddit and interviewed 20 participants (seven teenagers and 13 parents).

## 7. AI for Cybersecurity and Abuse Detection

- [My Model is Malware to You: Transforming AI Models into Malware by Abusing TensorFlow APIs](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2025/accepted-papers.html#collapse-84)
  - **Research question:** How does “My Model is Malware to You: Transforming AI Models into Malware by Abusing TensorFlow APIs” use AI to improve a conventional security or abuse-detection task?
  - **What they did:** To address these security concerns, the authors present a systematic analysis of the security risks associated with TensorFlow APIs.
