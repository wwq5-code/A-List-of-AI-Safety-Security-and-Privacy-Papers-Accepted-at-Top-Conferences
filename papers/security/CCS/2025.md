# CCS 2025

This list focuses on accepted papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems. Papers were screened by title from the [CCS 2025 official paper list](https://www.sigsac.org/ccs/CCS2025/accepted-papers/) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each `Research question` and `What they did` line is one sentence. Method summaries are grounded in the abstract; if no public abstract was available, the entry says so explicitly instead of inferring a contribution from the title.

## 1. LLM and Agent Security

- [Prompt Inference Attack on Distributed Large Language Model Inference Frameworks](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3719027.3744820)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Prompt Inference Attack on Distributed Large Language Model Inference Frameworks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors evaluate these attacks on a range of LLMs, including state-of-the-art models such as Llama-3.2 and Phi-3.5, as well as widely-used models like GPT-2 and BERT for comparative analysis.
- [Analyzing PDFs like Binaries: Adversarially Robust PDF Malware Analysis via Intermediate Representation and Language Model](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3719027.3744829)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Analyzing PDFs like Binaries: Adversarially Robust PDF Malware Analysis via Intermediate Representation and Language Model” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To tackle this, the authors propose a novel approach for PDF feature extraction and PDF malware detection.
- [SecAlign: Defending Against Prompt Injection with Preference Optimization](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3719027.3744836)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “SecAlign: Defending Against Prompt Injection with Preference Optimization” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To mitigate this vulnerability, the authors propose a new defense called SecAlign based on the technique of preference optimization.
- [Give LLMs a Security Course: Securing Retrieval-Augmented Code Generation via Knowledge Injection](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3719027.3765049)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Give LLMs a Security Course: Securing Retrieval-Augmented Code Generation via Knowledge Injection” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address this, the authors propose a security-hardening framework for RACG systems, CodeGuarder, that shifts the paradigm from retrieving only functional code examples to incorporating both functional code and security knowledge.

## 2. Backdoors, Poisoning, and Adversarial ML

- [On the Feasibility of Poisoning Text-to-Image AI Models via Adversarial Mislabeling](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3719027.3744845)
  - **Research question:** How does “On the Feasibility of Poisoning Text-to-Image AI Models via Adversarial Mislabeling” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Finally, the authors demonstrate the real-world effectiveness of these attacks, achieving high attack success (over 73%) even in black-box scenarios against commercial VLMs (Google Vertex AI and Microsoft Azure).
- [Towards Backdoor Stealthiness in Model Parameter Space](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3719027.3744846)
  - **Research question:** How does “Towards Backdoor Stealthiness in Model Parameter Space” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To answer this question, the authors examine 12 common backdoor attacks that focus on input-space or feature-space stealthiness and 17 diverse representative defenses.

  - **What they did:** To overcome the above shortcomings, this paper proposes a novel data-free backdoor defense approach for FL, named FilterFL, which strives to prevent uploaded client models with backdoor knowledge from participating in the aggregation operation in each FL communication…
- [On Hyperparameters and Backdoor-Resistance in Horizontal Federated Learning](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3719027.3765211)
  - **Research question:** How does “On Hyperparameters and Backdoor-Resistance in Horizontal Federated Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors study the impact of training hyperparameters on the effectiveness of backdoor attacks and defenses in HFL.

## 4. Secure and Verifiable AI Systems

- [Armadillo: Robust Single-Server Secure Aggregation for Federated Learning with Input Validation](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3719027.3765216)
  - **Research question:** How does “Armadillo: Robust Single-Server Secure Aggregation for Federated Learning with Input Validation” make AI training or inference more secure, private, or verifiable?
  - **What they did:** The authors present a secure aggregation system Armadillo that has disruptive resistance against adversarial clients, such that any coalition of malicious clients can affect the aggregation result only by misreporting their private inputs in a pre-defined legitimate range.
- [CryptGNN: Enabling Secure Inference for Graph Neural Networks](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3719027.3765232)
  - **Research question:** How does “CryptGNN: Enabling Secure Inference for Graph Neural Networks” make AI training or inference more secure, private, or verifiable?
  - **What they did:** The authors present CryptGNN, a secure and effective inference solution for third-party graph neural network (GNN) models in the cloud, which are accessed by clients as ML as a service (MLaaS).

## 5. Deepfakes, Watermarking, and Content Provenance

- [PoisonSpot: Precise Spotting of Clean-Label Backdoors via Fine-Grained Training Provenance Tracking](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3719027.3744802)
  - **Research question:** How does “PoisonSpot: Precise Spotting of Clean-Label Backdoors via Fine-Grained Training Provenance Tracking” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** The authors present PoisonSpot, a novel system that precisely detects clean-label backdoor attacks by using fine-grained training provenance tracking , inspired by dynamic taint tracking.
- [RAG-WM: An Efficient Black-Box Watermarking Approach for Retrieval-Augmented Generation of Large Language Models](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3719027.3744813)
  - **Research question:** How does “RAG-WM: An Efficient Black-Box Watermarking Approach for Retrieval-Augmented Generation of Large Language Models” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** To address those problems, the authors propose a novel black-box ''knowledge watermark'' approach, named RAG-WM, to detect IP infringement of RAGs.
- [Removal Attack and Defense on AI Generated Content Latent-based Watermarking](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3719027.3765175)
  - **Research question:** How does “Removal Attack and Defense on AI Generated Content Latent-based Watermarking” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** The authors demonstrate that indistinguishability alone does not necessarily guarantee resistance to adversarial removal.
- [PreferCare: Preference Dataset Copyright Protection in LLM Alignment by Watermark Injection and Verification](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3719027.3765223)
  - **Research question:** How does “PreferCare: Preference Dataset Copyright Protection in LLM Alignment by Watermark Injection and Verification” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose PreferCare, the first framework tailor-made for preference dataset copyright protection via watermark injection and verification.

## 6. AI Safety, Alignment, and Responsible AI

- [UnsafeBench: Benchmarking Image Safety Classifiers on Real-World and AI-Generated Images](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3719027.3765088)
  - **Research question:** How does “UnsafeBench: Benchmarking Image Safety Classifiers on Real-World and AI-Generated Images” evaluate or improve AI safety, alignment, oversight, or responsible deployment?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose UnsafeBench, a benchmarking framework that evaluates the effectiveness and robustness of image safety classifiers, with a particular focus on the impact of AI-generated images on their performance.

## 7. AI for Cybersecurity and Abuse Detection

- [Slot: Provenance-Driven APT Detection through Graph Reinforcement Learning](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3719027.3744788)
  - **Research question:** How does “Slot: Provenance-Driven APT Detection through Graph Reinforcement Learning” use AI to improve a conventional security or abuse-detection task?
  - **What they did:** To overcome these challenges, the authors propose Slot, an advanced APT detection approach based on provenance graphs and graph reinforcement learning.
- [Oedipus: LLM-enchanced Reasoning CAPTCHA Solver](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3719027.3744872)
  - **Research question:** How does “Oedipus: LLM-enchanced Reasoning CAPTCHA Solver” use AI to improve a conventional security or abuse-detection task?
  - **What they did:** Driven by the evolving AI capabilities, particularly the advancements in Large Language Models (LLMs), the authors investigate the potential of multimodal LLMs to solve modern reasoning CAPTCHAs.
- [Hardening Deep Neural Network Binaries against Reverse Engineering Attacks](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3719027.3765144)
  - **Research question:** How does “Hardening Deep Neural Network Binaries against Reverse Engineering Attacks” use AI to improve a conventional security or abuse-detection task?
  - **What they did:** The authors show that existing reverse engineering attacks on DNN binaries can recover 98.5% of DNN operators from DNN binaries that have been obfuscated using general obfuscators.

- [The Phantom Menace in PET-Hardened Deep Learning Models: Invisible Configuration-Induced Attacks](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3719027.3765107)
  - **Research question:** What AI security or privacy weakness does “The Phantom Menace in PET-Hardened Deep Learning Models: Invisible Configuration-Induced Attacks” expose or mitigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors present ConPETro, the first attack on CPET-hardened models by manipulating the CPET-DL framework configurations.
- [Safeguarding Graph Neural Networks against Topology Inference Attacks](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3719027.3765173)
  - **Research question:** What AI security or privacy weakness does “Safeguarding Graph Neural Networks against Topology Inference Attacks” expose or mitigate?
  - **What they did:** To this end, the authors propose a suite of Topology Inference Attacks (TIAs) that can reconstruct the structure of a target training graph using only black-box access to a GNN model.
