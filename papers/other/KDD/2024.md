# KDD 2024

This list focuses on accepted papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems. Papers were screened by title from the [KDD 2024 official paper list or proceedings](https://www.kdd.org/kdd2024/research-track-papers/) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each `Research question` and `What they did` line is one sentence, and every method summary is grounded in the paper abstract.

## 1. LLM and Agent Security

- [Fake News in Sheep’s Clothing: Robust Fake News Detection Against LLM-Empowered Style Attacks](https://doi.org/10.1145/3637528.3671977)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Fake News in Sheep’s Clothing: Robust Fake News Detection Against LLM-Empowered Style Attacks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address this, the authors introduce SheepDog, a style-robust fake news detector that prioritizes content over style in determining news veracity.

## 2. Backdoors, Poisoning, and Adversarial ML

- [FedRoLA: Robust Federated Learning Against Model Poisoning via Layer-based Aggregation](https://doi.org/10.1145/3637528.3671906)
  - **Research question:** How does “FedRoLA: Robust Federated Learning Against Model Poisoning via Layer-based Aggregation” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To bridge these gaps, the authors introduce FedRoLa, a cutting-edge similarity-based defense method optimized for FL.
- [Improving Robustness of Hyperbolic Neural Networks by Lipschitz Analysis](https://doi.org/10.1145/3637528.3671875)
  - **Research question:** How does “Improving Robustness of Hyperbolic Neural Networks by Lipschitz Analysis” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors conduct a rigorous Lipschitz analysis for HNNs and propose using Lipschitz regularization as a novel strategy to enhance their robustness.
- [Reinforced Compressive Neural Architecture Search for Versatile Adversarial Robustness](https://doi.org/10.1145/3637528.3672009)
  - **Research question:** How does “Reinforced Compressive Neural Architecture Search for Versatile Adversarial Robustness” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To address this key challenge, the authors propose Reinforced Compressive Neural Architecture Search (RC-NAS), aiming to achieve Versatile Adversarial Robustness.
- [Unveiling Vulnerabilities of Contrastive Recommender Systems to Poisoning Attacks](https://doi.org/10.1145/3637528.3671795)
  - **Research question:** How does “Unveiling Vulnerabilities of Contrastive Recommender Systems to Poisoning Attacks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Their analysis indicates that this vulnerability is attributed to the uniform spread of representations caused by the InfoNCE loss.
- [Rethinking Graph Backdoor Attacks: A Distribution-Preserving Perspective](https://doi.org/10.1145/3637528.3671910)
  - **Research question:** How does “Rethinking Graph Backdoor Attacks: A Distribution-Preserving Perspective” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Therefore, in this paper, the authors study a novel problem of unnoticeable graph backdoor attacks with in-distribution (ID) triggers.
- [Cross-Context Backdoor Attacks against Graph Prompt Learning](https://doi.org/10.1145/3637528.3671956)
  - **Research question:** How does “Cross-Context Backdoor Attacks against Graph Prompt Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce CrossBA, the first cross-context backdoor attack against GPL, which manipulates only the pretraining phase without requiring knowledge of downstream applications.

## 3. AI Privacy, Memorization, and Unlearning

- [FLea: Addressing Data Scarcity and Label Skew in Federated Learning via Privacy-preserving Feature Augmentation](https://doi.org/10.1145/3637528.3671899)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “FLea: Addressing Data Scarcity and Label Skew in Federated Learning via Privacy-preserving Feature Augmentation” investigate?
  - **What they did:** In response to these challenges, the authors propose a pioneering framework called FLea, incorporating the following key components: i) A global feature buffer that stores activation-target pairs shared from multiple clients to support local training.
- [Privacy-Preserved Neural Graph Databases](https://doi.org/10.1145/3637528.3671678)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Privacy-Preserved Neural Graph Databases” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a privacy-preserved neural graph database (P-NGDB) framework to alleviate the risks of privacy leakage in NGDBs.
- [Where Have You Been? A Study of Privacy Risk for Point-of-Interest Recommendation](https://doi.org/10.1145/3637528.3671758)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Where Have You Been? A Study of Privacy Risk for Point-of-Interest Recommendation” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To better understand and quantify the privacy leakage in mobility data-based ML models, the authors design a privacy attack suite containing data extraction and membership inference attacks tailored for point-of-interest (POI) recommendation models, one of the most widely used mobility data-based ML models.
- [Unveiling Privacy Vulnerabilities: Investigating the Role of Structure in Graph Data](https://doi.org/10.1145/3637528.3672013)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Unveiling Privacy Vulnerabilities: Investigating the Role of Structure in Graph Data” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To achieve this, the authors first investigate the problem of Graph Privacy Leakage via Structure (GPS), and introduce a novel measure, the Generalized Homophily Ratio, to quantify the various mechanisms contributing to privacy breach risks in GPS.
- [IDEA: A Flexible Framework of Certified Unlearning for Graph Neural Networks](https://doi.org/10.1145/3637528.3671744)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “IDEA: A Flexible Framework of Certified Unlearning for Graph Neural Networks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a principled framework named IDEA to achieve flexible and certified unlearning for GNNs.
