# KDD 2026

This list focuses on papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems themselves. Papers were screened by title from the [official 2026 paper list](https://kdd2026.kdd.org/papers/) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each summary below is based on the paper's public abstract. When no public abstract could be located, the entry is explicitly marked as unavailable rather than inferred from the title alone.

  - **Research question:** How does “AGMark: Attention-Guided Dynamic Watermarking for Large Vision-Language Models” improve generated-content detection, attribution, provenance, or ownership protection?
  - **What they did:** To address these challenges, the authors propose Attention-Guided Dynamic Watermarking (AGMark), a novel framework that embeds detectable signals while largely preserving visual-semantic fidelity.
