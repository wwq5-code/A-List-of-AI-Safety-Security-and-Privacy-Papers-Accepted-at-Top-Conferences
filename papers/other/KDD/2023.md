# KDD 2023

This list focuses on accepted papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems. Papers were screened by title from the [KDD 2023 official paper list or proceedings](https://dl.acm.org/doi/proceedings/10.1145/3580305) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each `Research question` and `What they did` line is one sentence, and every method summary is grounded in the paper abstract.

## 1. Backdoors, Poisoning, and Adversarial ML

- [Revisiting Personalized Federated Learning: Robustness Against Backdoor Attacks](https://doi.org/10.1145/3580305.3599898)
  - **Research question:** How does “Revisiting Personalized Federated Learning: Robustness Against Backdoor Attacks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Besides improving prediction accuracy, the authors study whether personalization could bring robustness benefits to backdoor attacks.
- [Investigating Trojan Attacks on Pre-trained Language Model-powered Database Middleware](https://doi.org/10.1145/3580305.3599395)
  - **Research question:** How does “Investigating Trojan Attacks on Pre-trained Language Model-powered Database Middleware” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors investigate the security threats posed by malicious PLMs to these emerging database middleware.

## 2. AI Privacy, Memorization, and Unlearning

- [Machine Unlearning in Gradient Boosting Decision Trees](https://doi.org/10.1145/3580305.3599420)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Machine Unlearning in Gradient Boosting Decision Trees” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors formalized the machine unlearning problem and its relaxed version.
- [A Unified Framework of Graph Information Bottleneck for Robustness and Membership Privacy](https://doi.org/10.1145/3580305.3599248)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “A Unified Framework of Graph Information Bottleneck for Robustness and Membership Privacy” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a graph information-bottleneck framework that reduces structural noise and uses pseudo-label optimization to improve both adversarial robustness and membership privacy.
- [Feature-based Learning for Diverse and Privacy-Preserving Counterfactual Explanations](https://doi.org/10.1145/3580305.3599343)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Feature-based Learning for Diverse and Privacy-Preserving Counterfactual Explanations” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors demonstrate the flexibility and effectiveness of their method in generating diverse counterfactuals of actionability and plausibility.
- [Privacy Matters: Vertical Federated Linear Contextual Bandits for Privacy Protected Recommendation](https://doi.org/10.1145/3580305.3599475)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Privacy Matters: Vertical Federated Linear Contextual Bandits for Privacy Protected Recommendation” investigate?
  - **What they did:** By conducting extensive experiments on both synthetic and real-world datasets, the authors show the superiority of the proposed method in terms of privacy protection and recommendation performance.
- [CampER: An Effective Framework for Privacy-Aware Deep Entity Resolution](https://doi.org/10.1145/3580305.3599266)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “CampER: An Effective Framework for Privacy-Aware Deep Entity Resolution” investigate?
  - **What they did:** Specifically, the authors first design a training pair self-generation strategy to overcome the absence of manually labeled data in privacy-aware scenarios.
- [FedSkill: Privacy Preserved Interpretable Skill Learning via Imitation](https://doi.org/10.1145/3580305.3599349)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “FedSkill: Privacy Preserved Interpretable Skill Learning via Imitation” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To this end, the authors present a privacy-preserved interpretable skill learning framework (FedSkill) that enables global policy learning to incorporate data from different sources and provides explainable interpretations to each local user without violating privacy and data sovereignty.
- [Certified Edge Unlearning for Graph Neural Networks](https://doi.org/10.1145/3580305.3599271)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Certified Edge Unlearning for Graph Neural Networks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To overcome this challenge, the authors introduce CEU, a Certified Edge Unlearning framework.
- [FedPseudo: Privacy-Preserving Pseudo Value-Based Deep Learning Models for Federated Survival Analysis](https://doi.org/10.1145/3580305.3599348)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “FedPseudo: Privacy-Preserving Pseudo Value-Based Deep Learning Models for Federated Survival Analysis” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a novel and simple approach for estimating pseudo values for FSA.
- [How does the Memorization of Neural Networks Impact Adversarial Robust Models?](https://doi.org/10.1145/3580305.3599381)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “How does the Memorization of Neural Networks Impact Adversarial Robust Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors study the effect of memorization in adversarial trained DNNs and disclose two important findings: (a) Memorizing atypical samples is only effective to improve DNN's accuracy on clean atypical samples, but hardly improve their adversarial robustness and (b) Memorizing certain atypical samples will even hurt the DNN's performance on typical samples.

## 3. AI Safety, Alignment, and Responsible AI

- [Reducing Exposure to Harmful Content via Graph Rewiring](https://doi.org/10.1145/3580305.3599489)
  - **Research question:** How does “Reducing Exposure to Harmful Content via Graph Rewiring” evaluate or improve AI safety, alignment, oversight, or responsible deployment?
  - **What they did:** Hence, modeling media items and recommendations as a directed graph, the authors study the problem of reducing the exposure to harmful content via edge rewiring.

## 4. Other AI Security and Privacy

- [FedDefender: Client-Side Attack-Tolerant Federated Learning](https://doi.org/10.1145/3580305.3599346)
  - **Research question:** What AI security or privacy weakness does “FedDefender: Client-Side Attack-Tolerant Federated Learning” expose or mitigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a new defense mechanism that focuses on the client-side, called FedDefender, to help benign clients train robust local models and avoid the adverse impact of malicious model updates from attackers, even when a server-side defense cannot identify or remove adversaries.
