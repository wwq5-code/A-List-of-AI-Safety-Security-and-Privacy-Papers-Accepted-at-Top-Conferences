# KDD 2025

This list focuses on accepted papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems. Papers were screened by title from the [KDD 2025 official paper list](https://kdd2025.kdd.org/research-track-papers-2/) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each `Research question` and `What they did` line is one sentence. Method summaries are grounded in the abstract; if no public abstract was available, the entry says so explicitly instead of inferring a contribution from the title.

## 1. LLM and Agent Security

- [Generalizable Graph Prompt Learning Framework with Model-level Prompt Injection and Two-Stage Prompt Tuning](https://doi.org/10.1145/3711896.3736976)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Generalizable Graph Prompt Learning Framework with Model-level Prompt Injection and Two-Stage Prompt Tuning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To overcome these limitations, the authors introduce the generalizable graph prompt learning framework (GGPL), which incorporates model-level prompt injection and a two-stage prompt tuning strategy.
- [Knowledge-to-Jailbreak: Investigating Knowledge-driven Jailbreaking Attacks for Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.1145/3711896.3737014)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Knowledge-to-Jailbreak: Investigating Knowledge-driven Jailbreaking Attacks for Large Language Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To bridge this gap, the authors propose a new task, knowledge-to-jailbreak, which aims to generate jailbreaking attacks from domain knowledge, requiring both attack effectiveness and knowledge relevance.
- [Benchmarking and Defending against Indirect Prompt Injection Attacks on Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.1145/3690624.3709179)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Benchmarking and Defending against Indirect Prompt Injection Attacks on Large Language Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address this critical yet under-explored issue, the authors introduce the first benchmark for bindirect prompt injection attacks, named BIPIA, to assess the risk of such vulnerabilities.
- [Can Large Language Models Improve the Adversarial Robustness of Graph Neural Networks?](https://doi.org/10.1145/3690624.3709256)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Can Large Language Models Improve the Adversarial Robustness of Graph Neural Networks?” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose an LLM-based robust graph structure inference framework, LLM4RGNN.
- [Stabilizing Modality Gap & Lowering Gradient Norms Improve Zero-Shot Adversarial Robustness of VLMs](https://doi.org/10.1145/3690624.3709296)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Stabilizing Modality Gap & Lowering Gradient Norms Improve Zero-Shot Adversarial Robustness of VLMs” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors show that maximizing the effective rank helps lower and stabilize the modality gap over adversaries with varying perturbation radii.

## 2. Backdoors, Poisoning, and Adversarial ML

- [Are You Using Reliable Graph Prompts? Trojan Prompt Attacks on Graph Neural Networks](https://doi.org/10.1145/3711896.3736845)
  - **Research question:** How does “Are You Using Reliable Graph Prompts? Trojan Prompt Attacks on Graph Neural Networks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To address the challenge of model fine-tuning by users, the authors introduce a finetuning-resistant poisoning approach that maintains the effectiveness of the backdoor even after downstream model adjustments.
- [Experience Speaks Louder: Black-box Hard-label Adversarial Attack through Reinforcement Learning](https://doi.org/10.1145/3711896.3736946)
  - **Research question:** How does “Experience Speaks Louder: Black-box Hard-label Adversarial Attack through Reinforcement Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To achieve query-efficient adversarial text generation, the authors propose EGAttack, an experience-guided black-box hard-label attack method based on reinforcement learning.
- [Revisiting Graph Adversarial Attack: A Perspective of Budget Optimization](https://doi.org/10.1145/3711896.3737107)
  - **Research question:** How does “Revisiting Graph Adversarial Attack: A Perspective of Budget Optimization” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To address this issue, this paper proposes Budget-Reduced Attack Filtering (BRAF in short) frameworks capable of eliminating ineffective perturbations produced by existing graph adversarial attack methods, thereby optimizing the attack budget.

## 3. AI Privacy, Memorization, and Unlearning

- [ALSA: Context-Sensitive Prompt Privacy Preservation in Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.1145/3711896.3736840)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “ALSA: Context-Sensitive Prompt Privacy Preservation in Large Language Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address these concerns, this paper introduces a context-sensitive prompt privacy-preserving framework, namely Adaptive Linguistic Sanitization and Anonymization (ALSA).
- [ATOM: A Framework of Detecting Query-Based Model Extraction Attacks for Graph Neural Networks](https://doi.org/10.1145/3711896.3736848)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “ATOM: A Framework of Detecting Query-Based Model Extraction Attacks for Graph Neural Networks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address these limitations, the authors propose ATOM, a novel real-time MEA detection framework tailored for GNNs.
- [From Expansion to Retraction: Long-tailed Machine Unlearning via Boundary Manipulation](https://doi.org/10.1145/3711896.3736970)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “From Expansion to Retraction: Long-tailed Machine Unlearning via Boundary Manipulation” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose the first Long-Tailed Machine Unlearning (LTMU) framework from a unified decision-boundary perspective.
- [GuardFGL: Similarity-driven Federated Graph Learning with Adversarial Robustness and Membership Privacy](https://doi.org/10.1145/3711896.3736994)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “GuardFGL: Similarity-driven Federated Graph Learning with Adversarial Robustness and Membership Privacy” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address these important challenges, the authors propose GuardFGL, a novel similarity-driven FGL that extracts minimal-sufficient information from polluted data to maintain strong adversarial robustness and protect membership privacy.
- [PARSIFAL: Private and Robust Sign Federated Learning](https://doi.org/10.1145/3711896.3737074)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “PARSIFAL: Private and Robust Sign Federated Learning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a novel FL protocol, PARSIFAL, to address privacy leakage and model poisoning threats.
- [Verification of Incomplete Graph Unlearning through Adversarial Perturbations](https://doi.org/10.1145/3711896.3737179)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Verification of Incomplete Graph Unlearning through Adversarial Perturbations” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce PANDA, the first probabilistic GU verification framework, to detect such cheating behaviors.
- [LLM-Eraser: Optimizing Large Language Model Unlearning through Selective Pruning](https://doi.org/10.1145/3690624.3709312)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “LLM-Eraser: Optimizing Large Language Model Unlearning through Selective Pruning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address this limitation, the authors introduce a novel two-stage approach, named LLM-Eraser, for selectively identifying and editing parameters specifically associated with undesirable knowledge.
- [$FLMarket:$ Enabling Privacy-preserved Pre-training Data Pricing for Federated Learning](https://doi.org/10.1145/3690624.3709346)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “$FLMarket:$ Enabling Privacy-preserved Pre-training Data Pricing for Federated Learning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose FLMarket that integrates a two-stage pricing mechanism with a security protocol to address the utility-privacy conflict.

## 4. Deepfakes, Watermarking, and Content Provenance

- [An Efficient White-box LLM Watermarking for IP Protection on Online Market Platforms](https://doi.org/10.1145/3711896.3736841)
  - **Research question:** How does “An Efficient White-box LLM Watermarking for IP Protection on Online Market Platforms” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose an efficient white-box LLM watermarking technique called ELLMark.
- [Data Watermarking for Sequential Recommender Systems](https://doi.org/10.1145/3711896.3736903)
  - **Research question:** How does “Data Watermarking for Sequential Recommender Systems” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** The authors present a method named Dataset Watermarking for Recommender Systems (DWRS) to address them.
- [PreGIP: Watermarking the Pretraining of Graph Neural Networks for Deep IP Protection](https://doi.org/10.1145/3711896.3737089)
  - **Research question:** How does “PreGIP: Watermarking the Pretraining of Graph Neural Networks for Deep IP Protection” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** Hence, in this work, the authors propose a novel framework named PreGIP to watermark the pretraining of GNN encoder for IP protection while maintaining the high-quality of the embedding space.
