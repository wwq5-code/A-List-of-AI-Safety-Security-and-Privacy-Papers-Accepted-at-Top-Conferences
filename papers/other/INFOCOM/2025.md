# INFOCOM 2025

This list focuses on accepted papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems. Papers were screened by title from the [INFOCOM 2025 official paper list](https://infocom2025.ieee-infocom.org/program/accepted-paper-list-main-conference) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each `Research question` and `What they did` line is one sentence. Method summaries are grounded in the abstract; if no public abstract was available, the entry says so explicitly instead of inferring a contribution from the title.

## 1. LLM and Agent Security

- [Joint Optimization of Prompt Security and System Performance in Edge-Cloud LLM Systems](https://doi.org/10.1109/INFOCOM55648.2025.11044720)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Joint Optimization of Prompt Security and System Performance in Edge-Cloud LLM Systems” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors formalize the problem of joint prompt detection, latency, and resource optimization into a multi-stage dynamic Bayesian game model.

## 2. Backdoors, Poisoning, and Adversarial ML

- [Protocol-agnostic and Data-free Backdoor Attacks on Pre-trained Models in RF Fingerprinting](https://doi.org/10.1109/INFOCOM55648.2025.11044704)
  - **Research question:** How does “Protocol-agnostic and Data-free Backdoor Attacks on Pre-trained Models in RF Fingerprinting” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors thoroughly investigate data-free backdoor attacks on such PTMs in RF fingerprinting, focusing on a practical scenario where attackers lack access to downstream data, label information, and training processes.

## 3. AI Privacy, Memorization, and Unlearning

- [Combating Deep Leakage from Gradients in Cross-Silo Federated Learning with QKD](https://doi.org/10.1109/INFOCOM55648.2025.11044743)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Combating Deep Leakage from Gradients in Cross-Silo Federated Learning with QKD” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose QuGrad, which encrypts each gradient with one secret bit while making the encrypted gradient vector orthogonal to the original.
- [AoI-aware Federated Unlearning for Streaming Data with Online Client Selection and Pricing](https://doi.org/10.1109/INFOCOM55648.2025.11044760)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “AoI-aware Federated Unlearning for Streaming Data with Online Client Selection and Pricing” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors design a federated unlearning algorithm, derive a Lyapunov-based threshold client-selection policy and a closed-form Nash equilibrium for client prices, and report up to 32.31% lower server cost.
- [Lightweight Federated Learning with Differential Privacy and Straggler Resilience](https://doi.org/10.1109/INFOCOM55648.2025.11044562)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Lightweight Federated Learning with Differential Privacy and Straggler Resilience” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose LightDP-FL, a novel lightweight scheme that ensures provable DP against untrusted peers and server, while maintaining straggler resilience, low overheads and high training accuracy.
- [Privacy-Preserving Wi-Fi Data Generation via Differential Privacy in Diffusion Models](https://doi.org/10.1109/INFOCOM55648.2025.11044673)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Privacy-Preserving Wi-Fi Data Generation via Differential Privacy in Diffusion Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a hybrid training method that first trains without privacy constraints, then applies differential privacy during fine-tuning with co-optimization to preserve utility, and evaluate it against state-of-the-art membership inference attacks.
- [Preference Profiling Attacks Against Vertical Federated Learning over Graph Data](https://doi.org/10.1109/INFOCOM55648.2025.11044459)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Preference Profiling Attacks Against Vertical Federated Learning over Graph Data” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose SGPP, which combines a compatible surrogate classifier, sensitivity extraction, a preference classifier, and an Adversarial Correction Block to profile class preferences across domains, and evaluate it under two attack scenarios on multiple graph datasets.

## 4. Secure and Verifiable AI Systems

- [Input Integrity and Authentic Results: Towards Trustworthy Aggregation in Federated Learning](https://doi.org/10.1109/INFOCOM55648.2025.11044719)
  - **Research question:** How does “Input Integrity and Authentic Results: Towards Trustworthy Aggregation in Federated Learning” make AI training or inference more secure, private, or verifiable?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose HyperSA, a two-round-trip trustworthy aggregation scheme that combines consistent authenticated decryption with a chain of three zero-knowledge proofs to guarantee input integrity and result authenticity, then implement and evaluate it against prior schemes.

## 5. Deepfakes, Watermarking, and Content Provenance

- [Exploring Acoustic Reverse Nonlinearity Against Speech Forgery in Real-time Voice Applications](https://doi.org/10.1109/INFOCOM55648.2025.11044526)
  - **Research question:** How does “Exploring Acoustic Reverse Nonlinearity Against Speech Forgery in Real-time Voice Applications” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose Aegis, which uses acoustic reverse nonlinearity to insert inaudible, speech-correlated ultrasonic credibility factors into live audio on commodity mobile devices, enabling later tamper detection.
- [AIGC-CM: An Efficient and Scalable Blockchain Solution for AIGC Copyright Management](https://doi.org/10.1109/INFOCOM55648.2025.11044774)
  - **Research question:** How does “AIGC-CM: An Efficient and Scalable Blockchain Solution for AIGC Copyright Management” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose AIGC-CM, combining a feature-based hash and structured metadata with a Hysteresis Signature DAG, formally analyze it in the UC framework, and evaluate a Fabric 2.4 prototype on 140 nodes.
