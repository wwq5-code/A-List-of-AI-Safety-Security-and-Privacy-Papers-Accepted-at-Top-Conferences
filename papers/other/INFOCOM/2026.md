# INFOCOM 2026

This list focuses on papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems themselves. Papers were screened by title from the [official 2026 paper list](https://infocom2026.ieee-infocom.org/accepted-paper-list-main-conference) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each summary below is based on the paper's public abstract. When no public abstract could be located, the entry is explicitly marked as unavailable rather than inferred from the title alone.

## 3. AI Privacy, Memorization, and Unlearning

- [Dual-Phase Federated Deep Unlearning via Weight-Aware Rollback and Reconstruction](https://doi.org/10.1109/infocom59046.2026.11571665)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, or forgetting problem does “Dual-Phase Federated Deep Unlearning via Weight-Aware Rollback and Reconstruction” expose or address?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce DPUL, a novel server-side unlearning method that deeply unlearns all influential weights to prevent privacy pitfalls.
- [PrivTune: Efficient and Privacy-Preserving Fine-Tuning of Large Language Models via Device-Cloud Collaboration](https://doi.org/10.1109/infocom59046.2026.11571688)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, or forgetting problem does “PrivTune: Efficient and Privacy-Preserving Fine-Tuning of Large Language Models via Device-Cloud Collaboration” expose or address?
  - **What they did:** To address this, the authors propose PrivTune, an efficient and privacy-preserving fine-tuning framework via Split Learning (SL).
- [Certified Unlearning in Decentralized Federated Learning](https://doi.org/10.1109/infocom59046.2026.11571416)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, or forgetting problem does “Certified Unlearning in Decentralized Federated Learning” expose or address?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a novel certified unlearning framework for DFL based on Newton-style updates.
- [AHE: Adaptive Homomorphic Encryption for Customizable Privacy in Heterogeneous Federated Learning](https://doi.org/10.1109/infocom59046.2026.11571699)

  - **What they did:** The authors propose AHE, a novel FL framework that integrates HE with adaptive privacy-aware clustering to dynamically optimize the latency-accuracy-privacy trade-off.
