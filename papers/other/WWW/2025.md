# The Web Conference (WWW) 2025

This list focuses on accepted papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems. Papers were screened by title from the [The Web Conference (WWW) 2025 official paper list](https://archives.iw3c2.org/www2025/www2025-proceedings.pdf) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each `Research question` and `What they did` line is one sentence. Method summaries are grounded in the abstract; if no public abstract was available, the entry says so explicitly instead of inferring a contribution from the title.

## 1. LLM and Agent Security

- [Dual Intention Escape: Penetrating and Toxic Jailbreak Attack against Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714654)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Dual Intention Escape: Penetrating and Toxic Jailbreak Attack against Large Language Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** Motivated by the mechanism in the psychology of human misjudgment, the authors propose a dual intention escape (DIE) jailbreak attack framework to generate more stealthy and toxic prompts to deceive LLMs to output harmful content.
- [You Can’t Eat Your Cake and Have It Too: The Performance Degradation of LLMs with Jailbreak Defense](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714632)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “You Can’t Eat Your Cake and Have It Too: The Performance Degradation of LLMs with Jailbreak Defense” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose USEBench, a novel benchmark designed to evaluate these aspects, along with USEIndex, a comprehensive metric for assessing overall model performance.

## 2. Backdoors, Poisoning, and Adversarial ML

- [Model Supply Chain Poisoning: Backdooring Pre-trained Models via Embedding Indistinguishability](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714624)
  - **Research question:** How does “Model Supply Chain Poisoning: Backdooring Pre-trained Models via Embedding Indistinguishability” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a novel and severer backdoor attack, TransTroj, which enables the backdoors embedded in PTMs to efficiently transfer in the model supply chain.
- [NI-GDBA: Non-Intrusive Distributed Backdoor Attack Based on Adaptive Perturbation on Federated Graph Learning](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714630)
  - **Research question:** How does “NI-GDBA: Non-Intrusive Distributed Backdoor Attack Based on Adaptive Perturbation on Federated Graph Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors also explore the robustness of NI-GDBA under different defense strategies, and based on their extensive experimental studies, the authors show that their attack method is robust to current federated learning defense methods, thus it is necessary…
- [SPEAR: A Structure-Preserving Manipulation Method for Graph Backdoor Attacks](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714665)
  - **Research question:** How does “SPEAR: A Structure-Preserving Manipulation Method for Graph Backdoor Attacks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To enhance the stealthiness of graph backdoors, the authors propose SPEAR, a novel structure-preserving graph backdoor attack that avoids modifying the graph's topology.
- [Revisiting Backdoor Attacks on Time Series Classification in the Frequency Domain](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714827)
  - **Research question:** How does “Revisiting Backdoor Attacks on Time Series Classification in the Frequency Domain” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To address this, the authors propose to generate triggers both effectively and efficiently, guided by frequency analysis.
- [Traceback of Poisoning Attacks to Retrieval-Augmented Generation](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714756)
  - **Research question:** How does “Traceback of Poisoning Attacks to Retrieval-Augmented Generation” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce RAGForensics, the first traceback system for RAG, designed to identify poisoned texts within the knowledge database that are responsible for the attacks.

## 3. AI Privacy, Memorization, and Unlearning

- [PM-MOE: Mixture of Experts on Private Model Parameters for Personalized Federated Learning](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714561)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “PM-MOE: Mixture of Experts on Private Model Parameters for Personalized Federated Learning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address these limitations, the authors propose PM-MoE architecture.
- [FLock: Robust and Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning based on Practical Blockchain State Channels](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714666)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “FLock: Robust and Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning based on Practical Blockchain State Channels” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose FLock, a robust and privacy-preserving FL scheme based on practical blockchain state channels.
- [Self-Comparison for Dataset-Level Membership Inference in Large (Vision-)Language Model](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714703)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Self-Comparison for Dataset-Level Membership Inference in Large (Vision-)Language Model” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a novel dataset-level membership inference method based on Self-Comparison.
- [Dynamic Graph Unlearning: A General and Efficient Post-Processing Method via Gradient Transformation](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714911)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Dynamic Graph Unlearning: A General and Efficient Post-Processing Method via Gradient Transformation” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To this end, the authors study the dynamic graph unlearning for the first time and propose an effective, efficient, general, and post-processing method to implement DGNN unlearning.
- [Subgraph Federated Unlearning](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714821)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Subgraph Federated Unlearning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To this end, in this paper, the authors propose ReGEnUnlearn, a subgraph federated unlearning framework for efficient and comprehensive unlearning of multiple target clients.
- [Unlearning Incentivizes Learning under Privacy Risk](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714740)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Unlearning Incentivizes Learning under Privacy Risk” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors formulate a set of contract design problems for both unlearning-disabled and unlearning-enabled scenarios.
- [Plug and Play: Enabling Pluggable Attribute Unlearning in Recommender Systems](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714671)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Plug and Play: Enabling Pluggable Attribute Unlearning in Recommender Systems” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors first identify three main challenges of current methods in dynamic environments, i.e., irreversible operation, low efficiency, and unsatisfied recommendation preservation.
- [Mask-based Membership Inference Attacks for Retrieval-Augmented Generation](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714771)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Mask-based Membership Inference Attacks for Retrieval-Augmented Generation” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address these limitations, the authors propose a Mask-Based Membership Inference Attacks (MBA) framework.
- [TAPE: Tailored Posterior Difference for Auditing of Machine Unlearning](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714875)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “TAPE: Tailored Posterior Difference for Auditing of Machine Unlearning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a TAilored Posterior diffErence (TAPE) method to provide unlearning auditing independently of original model training.
- [FUNU: Boosting Machine Unlearning Efficiency by Filtering Unnecessary Unlearning](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714711)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “FUNU: Boosting Machine Unlearning Efficiency by Filtering Unnecessary Unlearning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors first propose the concept of unnecessary unlearning, which indicates that the model would not alter noticeably after removing some data points.
- [Achieving Personalized Privacy-Preserving Graph Neural Network via Topology Awareness](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714555)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Achieving Personalized Privacy-Preserving Graph Neural Network via Topology Awareness” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address this issue, the authors propose a Topology-aware Differential Privacy Graph Neural Network learning framework, termed TDP-GNN, which can achieve personalized privacy protection for each node with improved privacy-utility guarantees.
- [Aegis: Post-Training Attribute Unlearning in Federated Recommender Systems against Attribute Inference Attacks](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714823)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Aegis: Post-Training Attribute Unlearning in Federated Recommender Systems against Attribute Inference Attacks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce a novel post-training federated unlearning framework, Aegis, which performs unlearning based on private attribute requests after the model has been trained, minimizing the degradation in recommendation accuracy.
- [MER-Inspector: Assessing Model Extraction Risks from An Attack-Agnostic Perspective](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714894)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “MER-Inspector: Assessing Model Extraction Risks from An Attack-Agnostic Perspective” investigate?
  - **What they did:** By using the Neural Tangent Kernel (NTK) theory, the authors formulate the linearized MEA as a regularized kernel classification problem and then derive the fidelity gap and generalization error bounds of the attack performance.
- [Towards Safe Machine Unlearning: A Paradigm that Mitigates Performance Degradation . 4635](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714638)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Towards Safe Machine Unlearning: A Paradigm that Mitigates Performance Degradation . 4635” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors study the machine unlearning problem which aims to remove specific training data from a pre-trained machine learning model to allow users to exercise their 'right to be forgotten' to protect user privacy.
- [Effectiveness of Privacy-preserving Algorithms in LLMs: A Benchmark and Empirical Analysis](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714531)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Effectiveness of Privacy-preserving Algorithms in LLMs: A Benchmark and Empirical Analysis” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address these challenges, the authors introduce ''Privacy-preserving4LLM Benchmarking'', a systematic evaluation framework that systematically assesses different privacy-preserving algorithms' utility-privacy trade-offs across different LLM architectures.

## 4. Deepfakes, Watermarking, and Content Provenance

- [Beyond Dataset Watermarking: Model-Level Copyright Protection for Code Summarization Models](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714641)
  - **Research question:** How does “Beyond Dataset Watermarking: Model-Level Copyright Protection for Code Summarization Models” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** To tackle these issues, the authors propose ModMark, a novel model-level digital watermark embedding method.

## 5. AI Safety, Alignment, and Responsible AI

- [Responsible Diffusion Models via Constraining Text Embeddings within Safe Regions](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714912)
  - **Research question:** How does “Responsible Diffusion Models via Constraining Text Embeddings within Safe Regions” evaluate or improve AI safety, alignment, oversight, or responsible deployment?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a novel self-discovery approach to identifying a semantic direction vector in the embedding space to restrict text embedding within a safe region.
