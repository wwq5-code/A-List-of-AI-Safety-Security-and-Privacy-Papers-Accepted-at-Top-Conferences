# CVPR 2024

This list focuses on accepted papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems. Papers were screened by title from the [CVPR 2024 official paper list or proceedings](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/CVPR2024?day=all) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each `Research question` and `What they did` line is one sentence, and every method summary is grounded in the paper abstract.

## 1. LLM and Agent Security

- [One Prompt Word is Enough to Boost Adversarial Robustness for Pre-trained Vision-Language Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Li_One_Prompt_Word_is_Enough_to_Boost_Adversarial_Robustness_for_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “One Prompt Word is Enough to Boost Adversarial Robustness for Pre-trained Vision-Language Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors first show that the effectiveness of both adversarial attack and defense are sensitive to the used text prompt.

## 2. Backdoors, Poisoning, and Adversarial ML

- [Transferable Structural Sparse Adversarial Attack Via Exact Group Sparsity Training](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Ming_Transferable_Structural_Sparse_Adversarial_Attack_Via_Exact_Group_Sparsity_Training_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Transferable Structural Sparse Adversarial Attack Via Exact Group Sparsity Training” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Leveraging this insight the authors introduce the structural sparsity constraint in the framework of generative models to limit the perturbation positions.
- [LOTUS: Evasive and Resilient Backdoor Attacks through Sub-Partitioning](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Cheng_LOTUS_Evasive_and_Resilient_Backdoor_Attacks_through_Sub-Partitioning_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “LOTUS: Evasive and Resilient Backdoor Attacks through Sub-Partitioning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To address and enhance both evasiveness and resilience the authors introduce a novel backdoor attack LOTUS.
- [Nearest is Not Dearest: Towards Practical Defense against Quantization-conditioned Backdoor Attacks](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Li_Nearest_is_Not_Dearest_Towards_Practical_Defense_against_Quantization-conditioned_Backdoor_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Nearest is Not Dearest: Towards Practical Defense against Quantization-conditioned Backdoor Attacks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors conduct the first in-depth analysis of QCBs.
- [Not All Prompts Are Secure: A Switchable Backdoor Attack Against Pre-trained Vision Transfomers](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Yang_Not_All_Prompts_Are_Secure_A_Switchable_Backdoor_Attack_Against_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Not All Prompts Are Secure: A Switchable Backdoor Attack Against Pre-trained Vision Transfomers” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors identify a novel security threat towards such a paradigm from the perspective of backdoor attacks.
- [Towards Understanding and Improving Adversarial Robustness of Vision Transformers](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Jain_Towards_Understanding_and_Improving_Adversarial_Robustness_of_Vision_Transformers_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Towards Understanding and Improving Adversarial Robustness of Vision Transformers” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors demonstrate that due to the softmax computations in every attention block in VITs they are inherently vulnerable to floating point underflow errors.
- [BadCLIP: Trigger-Aware Prompt Learning for Backdoor Attacks on CLIP](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Bai_BadCLIP_Trigger-Aware_Prompt_Learning_for_Backdoor_Attacks_on_CLIP_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “BadCLIP: Trigger-Aware Prompt Learning for Backdoor Attacks on CLIP” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Their method named BadCLIP is built on a novel and effective mechanism in backdoor attacks on CLIP i.e. influencing both the image and text encoders with the trigger.
- [Soften to Defend: Towards Adversarial Robustness via Self-Guided Label Refinement](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Li_Soften_to_Defend_Towards_Adversarial_Robustness_via_Self-Guided_Label_Refinement_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Soften to Defend: Towards Adversarial Robustness via Self-Guided Label Refinement” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors first identify a connection between robust overfitting and the excessive memorization of noisy labels in AT from a view of gradient norm.
- [Towards Transferable Targeted 3D Adversarial Attack in the Physical World](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Huang_Towards_Transferable_Targeted_3D_Adversarial_Attack_in_the_Physical_World_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Towards Transferable Targeted 3D Adversarial Attack in the Physical World” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To achieve this goal the authors design a novel framework named TT3D that could rapidly reconstruct from few multi-view images into Transferable Targeted 3D textured meshes.
- [Adversarial Backdoor Attack by Naturalistic Data Poisoning on Trajectory Prediction in Autonomous Driving](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Pourkeshavarz_Adversarial_Backdoor_Attack_by_Naturalistic_Data_Poisoning_on_Trajectory_Prediction_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Adversarial Backdoor Attack by Naturalistic Data Poisoning on Trajectory Prediction in Autonomous Driving” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors show that the proposed attack is highly effective as it can significantly hinder the performance of prediction models unnoticeable by the victims and efficient as it forces the victim to generate malicious behavior even under constrained conditions.
- [Physical Backdoor: Towards Temperature-based Backdoor Attacks in the Physical World](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Yin_Physical_Backdoor_Towards_Temperature-based_Backdoor_Attacks_in_the_Physical_World_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Physical Backdoor: Towards Temperature-based Backdoor Attacks in the Physical World” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** In the digital realm the authors evaluate their approach using benchmark datasets for TIOD achieving an Attack Success Rate (ASR) of up to 98.21%.
- [Backdoor Defense via Test-Time Detecting and Repairing](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Guan_Backdoor_Defense_via_Test-Time_Detecting_and_Repairing_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Backdoor Defense via Test-Time Detecting and Repairing” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors investigate the possibility of defending against backdoor attacks by utilizing test-time partially poisoned data to remove the backdoor from the model.
- [Language-Driven Anchors for Zero-Shot Adversarial Robustness](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Li_Language-Driven_Anchors_for_Zero-Shot_Adversarial_Robustness_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Language-Driven Anchors for Zero-Shot Adversarial Robustness” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors investigate this domain by leveraging the recent advances in large vision-language models such as CLIP to introduce zero-shot adversarial robustness to DNNs.
- [SlowFormer: Adversarial Attack on Compute and Energy Consumption of Efficient Vision Transformers](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Navaneet_SlowFormer_Adversarial_Attack_on_Compute_and_Energy_Consumption_of_Efficient_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “SlowFormer: Adversarial Attack on Compute and Energy Consumption of Efficient Vision Transformers” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors show that such models can be vulnerable to a universal adversarial patch attack where the attacker optimizes for a patch that when pasted on any image can increase the compute and power consumption of the model.
- [Semantic Shield: Defending Vision-Language Models Against Backdooring and Poisoning via Fine-grained Knowledge Alignment](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Ishmam_Semantic_Shield_Defending_Vision-Language_Models_Against_Backdooring_and_Poisoning_via_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Semantic Shield: Defending Vision-Language Models Against Backdooring and Poisoning via Fine-grained Knowledge Alignment” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a method for mitigating such attacks on contrastively trained vision-language models.
- [Pre-trained Model Guided Fine-Tuning for Zero-Shot Adversarial Robustness](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Wang_Pre-trained_Model_Guided_Fine-Tuning_for_Zero-Shot_Adversarial_Robustness_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Pre-trained Model Guided Fine-Tuning for Zero-Shot Adversarial Robustness” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose Pre-trained Model Guided Adversarial Fine-Tuning (PMG-AFT) method which leverages supervision from the original pre-trained model by carefully designing an auxiliary branch to enhance the model's zero-shot adversarial robustness.
- [BadCLIP: Dual-Embedding Guided Backdoor Attack on Multimodal Contrastive Learning](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Liang_BadCLIP_Dual-Embedding_Guided_Backdoor_Attack_on_Multimodal_Contrastive_Learning_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “BadCLIP: Dual-Embedding Guided Backdoor Attack on Multimodal Contrastive Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To achieve this the authors draw motivations from the perspective of the Bayesian rule and propose a dual-embedding guided framework for backdoor attacks.
- [Deep-TROJ: An Inference Stage Trojan Insertion Algorithm through Efficient Weight Replacement Attack](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Ahmed_Deep-TROJ_An_Inference_Stage_Trojan_Insertion_Algorithm_through_Efficient_Weight_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Deep-TROJ: An Inference Stage Trojan Insertion Algorithm through Efficient Weight Replacement Attack” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors develop a novel Trojan attack by adopting a unique memory fault injection technique that can inject bit-flip into the page table of the main memory.
- [Attack To Defend: Exploiting Adversarial Attacks for Detecting Poisoned Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Fares_Attack_To_Defend_Exploiting_Adversarial_Attacks_for_Detecting_Poisoned_Models_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Attack To Defend: Exploiting Adversarial Attacks for Detecting Poisoned Models” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a metric called sensitivity to adversarial perturbations (SAP) to measure the sensitivity of a ML model to adversarial attacks at a specific perturbation bound.
- [BrainWash: A Poisoning Attack to Forget in Continual Learning](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Abbasi_BrainWash_A_Poisoning_Attack_to_Forget_in_Continual_Learning_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “BrainWash: A Poisoning Attack to Forget in Continual Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce "BrainWash" a novel data poisoning method tailored to impose forgetting on a continual learner.
- [Data Poisoning based Backdoor Attacks to Contrastive Learning](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Zhang_Data_Poisoning_based_Backdoor_Attacks_to_Contrastive_Learning_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Data Poisoning based Backdoor Attacks to Contrastive Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Moreover the authors also propose a defense called localized cropping to defend against DPBAs.

## 3. AI Privacy, Memorization, and Unlearning

- [In-distribution Public Data Synthesis with Diffusion Models for Differentially Private Image Classification](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Park_In-distribution_Public_Data_Synthesis_with_Diffusion_Models_for_Differentially_Private_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “In-distribution Public Data Synthesis with Diffusion Models for Differentially Private Image Classification” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors investigate a framework that leverages recent diffusion models to amplify the information of public data.
- [Dual-Consistency Model Inversion for Non-Exemplar Class Incremental Learning](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Qiu_Dual-Consistency_Model_Inversion_for_Non-Exemplar_Class_Incremental_Learning_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Dual-Consistency Model Inversion for Non-Exemplar Class Incremental Learning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors present a novel method termed Dual-Consistency Model Inversion (DCMI) to generate better synthetic samples of old classes through two pivotal consistency alignments: (1) the semantic consistency between the synthetic images and the corresponding prototypes and (2) domain consistency between synthetic and real images of new classes.
- [Privacy-Preserving Optics for Enhancing Protection in Face De-Identification](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Lopez_Privacy-Preserving_Optics_for_Enhancing_Protection_in_Face_De-Identification_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Privacy-Preserving Optics for Enhancing Protection in Face De-Identification” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a hardware-level face de-identification method to solve this vulnerability.
- [PolarRec: Improving Radio Interferometric Data Reconstruction Using Polar Coordinates](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Wang_PolarRec_Improving_Radio_Interferometric_Data_Reconstruction_Using_Polar_Coordinates_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “PolarRec: Improving Radio Interferometric Data Reconstruction Using Polar Coordinates” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address these problems the authors propose PolarRec a transformer-encoder-conditioned reconstruction pipeline with visibility samples converted into the polar coordinate system.
- [Towards Memorization-Free Diffusion Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Chen_Towards_Memorization-Free_Diffusion_Models_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Towards Memorization-Free Diffusion Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address this the authors introduce Anti-Memorization Guidance (AMG) a novel framework employing three targeted guidance strategies for the main causes of memorization: image and caption duplication and highly specific user prompts.
- [Collaborative Learning of Anomalies with Privacy (CLAP) for Unsupervised Video Anomaly Detection: A New Baseline](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Al-lahham_Collaborative_Learning_of_Anomalies_with_Privacy_CLAP_for_Unsupervised_Video_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Collaborative Learning of Anomalies with Privacy (CLAP) for Unsupervised Video Anomaly Detection: A New Baseline” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a new baseline for anomaly detection capable of localizing anomalous events in complex surveillance scenarios in a fully unsupervised fashion without any labels on a privacy-retaining participant-based distributed training configuration.
- [CGI-DM: Digital Copyright Authentication for Diffusion Models via Contrasting Gradient Inversion](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Wu_CGI-DM_Digital_Copyright_Authentication_for_Diffusion_Models_via_Contrasting_Gradient_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “CGI-DM: Digital Copyright Authentication for Diffusion Models via Contrasting Gradient Inversion” investigate?
  - **What they did:** In response the authors present Contrasting Gradient Inversion for Diffusion Models (CGI-DM) a novel method featuring vivid visual representations for digital copyright authentication.
- [Model Inversion Robustness: Can Transfer Learning Help?](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Ho_Model_Inversion_Robustness_Can_Transfer_Learning_Help_CVPR_2024_paper.html)

  - **What they did:** The authors conduct an analysis using Fisher Information to justify their method.

  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Efficient Privacy-Preserving Visual Localization Using 3D Ray Clouds” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To overcome this limitation the authors propose a new lifting approach called "ray cloud" whereby each lifted 3D line intersects at one of two predefined locations depicting omnidirectional rays from two cameras.
- [DiffAM: Diffusion-based Adversarial Makeup Transfer for Facial Privacy Protection](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Sun_DiffAM_Diffusion-based_Adversarial_Makeup_Transfer_for_Facial_Privacy_Protection_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “DiffAM: Diffusion-based Adversarial Makeup Transfer for Facial Privacy Protection” investigate?

- [Privacy-Preserving Face Recognition Using Trainable Feature Subtraction](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Mi_Privacy-Preserving_Face_Recognition_Using_Trainable_Feature_Subtraction_CVPR_2024_paper.html)

- [Selective Interpretable and Motion Consistent Privacy Attribute Obfuscation for Action Recognition](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Ilic_Selective_Interpretable_and_Motion_Consistent_Privacy_Attribute_Obfuscation_for_Action_CVPR_2024_paper.html)

  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Generative Unlearning for Any Identity” investigate?

- [Validating Privacy-Preserving Face Recognition under a Minimum Assumption](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Zhang_Validating_Privacy-Preserving_Face_Recognition_under_a_Minimum_Assumption_CVPR_2024_paper.html)

- [OpticalDR: A Deep Optical Imaging Model for Privacy-Protective Depression Recognition](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Pan_OpticalDR_A_Deep_Optical_Imaging_Model_for_Privacy-Protective_Depression_Recognition_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “OpticalDR: A Deep Optical Imaging Model for Privacy-Protective Depression Recognition” investigate?
  - **What they did:** In order to mitigate the potential risks associated with the inappropriate disclosure of patient facial images the authors design a new imaging system to erase the identity information of captured facial images while retain disease-relevant features.
- [SimAC: A Simple Anti-Customization Method for Protecting Face Privacy against Text-to-Image Synthesis of Diffusion Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Wang_SimAC_A_Simple_Anti-Customization_Method_for_Protecting_Face_Privacy_against_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
