# CVPR 2023

This list focuses on accepted papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems. Papers were screened by title from the [CVPR 2023 official paper list or proceedings](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/CVPR2023?day=all) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each `Research question` and `What they did` line is one sentence, and every method summary is grounded in the paper abstract.

## 1. Backdoors, Poisoning, and Adversarial ML

- [TrojViT: Trojan Insertion in Vision Transformers](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Zheng_TrojViT_Trojan_Insertion_in_Vision_Transformers_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “TrojViT: Trojan Insertion in Vision Transformers” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a stealth and practical ViT-specific backdoor attack TrojViT.
- [Towards Compositional Adversarial Robustness: Generalizing Adversarial Training to Composite Semantic Perturbations](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Hsiung_Towards_Compositional_Adversarial_Robustness_Generalizing_Adversarial_Training_to_Composite_Semantic_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Towards Compositional Adversarial Robustness: Generalizing Adversarial Training to Composite Semantic Perturbations” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors first propose a novel method for generating composite adversarial examples.
- [Revisiting Residual Networks for Adversarial Robustness](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Huang_Revisiting_Residual_Networks_for_Adversarial_Robustness_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Revisiting Residual Networks for Adversarial Robustness” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** In both cases, the authors first derive insights through systematic experiments.
- [How to Backdoor Diffusion Models?](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Chou_How_to_Backdoor_Diffusion_Models_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “How to Backdoor Diffusion Models” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Specifically, the authors propose BadDiffusion, a novel attack framework that engineers compromised diffusion processes during model training for backdoor implantation.
- [Backdoor Defense via Deconfounded Representation Learning](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Zhang_Backdoor_Defense_via_Deconfounded_Representation_Learning_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Backdoor Defense via Deconfounded Representation Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors first construct a causal graph to model the generation process of poisoned data and find that the backdoor attack acts as the confounder, which brings spurious associations between the input images and target labels, making the model predictions less reliable.
- [Color Backdoor: A Robust Poisoning Attack in Color Space](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Jiang_Color_Backdoor_A_Robust_Poisoning_Attack_in_Color_Space_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Color Backdoor: A Robust Poisoning Attack in Color Space” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors present a novel color backdoor attack, which can exhibit robustness and stealthiness at the same time.
- [Backdoor Cleansing With Unlabeled Data](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Pang_Backdoor_Cleansing_With_Unlabeled_Data_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Backdoor Cleansing With Unlabeled Data” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** In experiments, the authors show that their method, trained without labels, is on-par with state-of-the-art defense methods trained using labels.
- [TWINS: A Fine-Tuning Framework for Improved Transferability of Adversarial Robustness and Generalization](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Liu_TWINS_A_Fine-Tuning_Framework_for_Improved_Transferability_of_Adversarial_Robustness_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “TWINS: A Fine-Tuning Framework for Improved Transferability of Adversarial Robustness and Generalization” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors study the model-based and data-based approaches for this goal and find that the two common approaches cannot achieve the objective of improving both generalization and adversarial robustness.
- [Proximal Splitting Adversarial Attack for Semantic Segmentation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Rony_Proximal_Splitting_Adversarial_Attack_for_Semantic_Segmentation_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Proximal Splitting Adversarial Attack for Semantic Segmentation” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors demonstrate that their attack significantly outperforms previously proposed ones, as well as classification attacks that the authors adapted for segmentation, providing a first comprehensive benchmark for this dense task.
- [Turning Strengths Into Weaknesses: A Certified Robustness Inspired Attack Framework Against Graph Neural Networks](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Wang_Turning_Strengths_Into_Weaknesses_A_Certified_Robustness_Inspired_Attack_Framework_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Turning Strengths Into Weaknesses: A Certified Robustness Inspired Attack Framework Against Graph Neural Networks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Accordingly, the authors design a certified robustness inspired attack loss, when incorporated into (any) existing attacks, produces their certified robustness inspired attack framework.
- [Discrete Point-Wise Attack Is Not Enough: Generalized Manifold Adversarial Attack for Face Recognition](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Li_Discrete_Point-Wise_Attack_Is_Not_Enough_Generalized_Manifold_Adversarial_Attack_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Discrete Point-Wise Attack Is Not Enough: Generalized Manifold Adversarial Attack for Face Recognition” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors demonstrate the effectiveness of their method based on extensive experiments, and reveal that GMAA promises a semantic continuous adversarial space with a higher generalization ability and visual quality.
- [TrojDiff: Trojan Attacks on Diffusion Models With Diverse Targets](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Chen_TrojDiff_Trojan_Attacks_on_Diffusion_Models_With_Diverse_Targets_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “TrojDiff: Trojan Attacks on Diffusion Models With Diverse Targets” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To answer these questions, the authors propose an effective Trojan attack against diffusion models, TrojDiff, which optimizes the Trojan diffusion and generative processes during training.
- [The Dark Side of Dynamic Routing Neural Networks: Towards Efficiency Backdoor Injection](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Chen_The_Dark_Side_of_Dynamic_Routing_Neural_Networks_Towards_Efficiency_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “The Dark Side of Dynamic Routing Neural Networks: Towards Efficiency Backdoor Injection” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To address this question, the authors propose EfficFrog, an adversarial attack that injects universal efficiency backdoors in DyNNs.
- [Backdoor Defense via Adaptively Splitting Poisoned Dataset](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Gao_Backdoor_Defense_via_Adaptively_Splitting_Poisoned_Dataset_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Backdoor Defense via Adaptively Splitting Poisoned Dataset” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Under their framework, the authors propose an adaptively splitting dataset-based defense (ASD).
- [Backdoor Attacks Against Deep Image Compression via Adaptive Frequency Trigger](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Tan_Backdoor_Attacks_Against_Deep_Image_Compression_via_Adaptive_Frequency_Trigger_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Backdoor Attacks Against Deep Image Compression via Adaptive Frequency Trigger” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors present a novel backdoor attack with multiple triggers against learned image compression models.
- [Black-Box Sparse Adversarial Attack via Multi-Objective Optimisation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Williams_Black-Box_Sparse_Adversarial_Attack_via_Multi-Objective_Optimisation_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Black-Box Sparse Adversarial Attack via Multi-Objective Optimisation” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To address these limitations, the authors propose a novel multi-objective sparse attack algorithm that efficiently minimizes the number of modified pixels and their size during the attack process.
- [Progressive Backdoor Erasing via Connecting Backdoor and Adversarial Attacks](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Mu_Progressive_Backdoor_Erasing_via_Connecting_Backdoor_and_Adversarial_Attacks_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Progressive Backdoor Erasing via Connecting Backdoor and Adversarial Attacks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors empirically show that, against 5 state-of-the-art backdoor attacks, their AFT can effectively erase the backdoor triggers without obvious performance degradation on clean samples and significantly outperforms existing defense methods.
- [Feature Separation and Recalibration for Adversarial Robustness](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Kim_Feature_Separation_and_Recalibration_for_Adversarial_Robustness_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Feature Separation and Recalibration for Adversarial Robustness” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To this end, the authors propose a novel, easy-to-plugin approach named Feature Separation and Recalibration (FSR) that recalibrates the malicious, non-robust activations for more robust feature maps through Separation and Recalibration.
- [ImageNet-E: Benchmarking Neural Network Robustness via Attribute Editing](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Li_ImageNet-E_Benchmarking_Neural_Network_Robustness_via_Attribute_Editing_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “ImageNet-E: Benchmarking Neural Network Robustness via Attribute Editing” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Instead of following the traditional research paradigm that investigates new out-of-distribution corruptions or perturbations deep models may encounter, the authors conduct model debugging in in-distribution data to explore which object attributes a model may be sensitive to.
- [You Are Catching My Attention: Are Vision Transformers Bad Learners Under Backdoor Attacks?](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Yuan_You_Are_Catching_My_Attention_Are_Vision_Transformers_Bad_Learners_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “You Are Catching My Attention: Are Vision Transformers Bad Learners Under Backdoor Attacks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Furthermore, the authors propose invisible variants of BadViT to increase the stealth of the attack by limiting the strength of the trigger perturbation.
- [Adversarial Robustness via Random Projection Filters](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Dong_Adversarial_Robustness_via_Random_Projection_Filters_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Adversarial Robustness via Random Projection Filters” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Taking advantage of the properties of random projection, the authors propose to replace part of convolutional filters with random projection filters, and theoretically explore the geometric representation preservation of proposed synthesized filters via Johnson-Lindenstrauss lemma.
- [Defending Against Patch-Based Backdoor Attacks on Self-Supervised Learning](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Tejankar_Defending_Against_Patch-Based_Backdoor_Attacks_on_Self-Supervised_Learning_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Defending Against Patch-Based Backdoor Attacks on Self-Supervised Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Moreover, the authors show that PatchSearch outperforms baselines and state-of-the-art defense approaches including those using additional clean, trusted data.
- [Single Image Backdoor Inversion via Robust Smoothed Classifiers](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Sun_Single_Image_Backdoor_Inversion_via_Robust_Smoothed_Classifiers_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Single Image Backdoor Inversion via Robust Smoothed Classifiers” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Last, the authors propose and analyze two countermeasures to their approach and show that SmoothInv remains robust in the face of an adaptive attacker.

## 2. AI Privacy, Memorization, and Unlearning

- [Re-Thinking Model Inversion Attacks Against Deep Neural Networks](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Nguyen_Re-Thinking_Model_Inversion_Attacks_Against_Deep_Neural_Networks_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Re-Thinking Model Inversion Attacks Against Deep Neural Networks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors analyze weaknesses in existing model-inversion objectives and MI overfitting, then propose an improved objective and model augmentation to strengthen the attacks.
- [Learning To Generate Image Embeddings With User-Level Differential Privacy](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Xu_Learning_To_Generate_Image_Embeddings_With_User-Level_Differential_Privacy_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Learning To Generate Image Embeddings With User-Level Differential Privacy” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To achieve user-level DP for large image-to-embedding feature extractors, the authors propose DP-FedEmb, a variant of federated learning algorithms with per-user sensitivity control and noise addition, to train from user-partitioned data centralized in datacenter.
- [The Resource Problem of Using Linear Layer Leakage Attack in Federated Learning](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Zhao_The_Resource_Problem_of_Using_Linear_Layer_Leakage_Attack_in_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “The Resource Problem of Using Linear Layer Leakage Attack in Federated Learning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors show that this results in a resource overhead which grows larger with an increasing number of clients.
- [ERM-KTP: Knowledge-Level Machine Unlearning via Knowledge Transfer](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Lin_ERM-KTP_Knowledge-Level_Machine_Unlearning_via_Knowledge_Transfer_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “ERM-KTP: Knowledge-Level Machine Unlearning via Knowledge Transfer” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address the above problems, the authors try to define machine unlearning from the knowledge perspective, and the authors propose a knowledge-level machine unlearning method, namely ERM-KTP.
- [Boundary Unlearning: Rapid Forgetting of Deep Networks via Shifting the Decision Boundary](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Chen_Boundary_Unlearning_Rapid_Forgetting_of_Deep_Networks_via_Shifting_the_CVPR_2023_paper.html)

  - **What they did:** The authors develop two novel boundary shift methods, namely Boundary Shrink and Boundary Expanding, both of which can rapidly achieve the utility and privacy guarantees.
- [Privacy-Preserving Adversarial Facial Features](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Wang_Privacy-Preserving_Adversarial_Facial_Features_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Privacy-Preserving Adversarial Facial Features” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To this end, the authors design a shadow model which simulates the attackers' behavior to capture the mapping function from facial features to images and generate adversarial latent noise to disrupt the mapping.
- [DartBlur: Privacy Preservation With Detection Artifact Suppression](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Jiang_DartBlur_Privacy_Preservation_With_Detection_Artifact_Suppression_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “DartBlur: Privacy Preservation With Detection Artifact Suppression” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a novel De-artifact Blurring(DartBlur) privacy-preserving method, which capitalizes on a DNN architecture to generate blurred faces.
- [Paired-Point Lifting for Enhanced Privacy-Preserving Visual Localization](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Lee_Paired-Point_Lifting_for_Enhanced_Privacy-Preserving_Visual_Localization_CVPR_2023_paper.html)

- [Make Landscape Flatter in Differentially Private Federated Learning](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Shi_Make_Landscape_Flatter_in_Differentially_Private_Federated_Learning_CVPR_2023_paper.html)

  - **What they did:** To alleviate these issues, the authors propose a novel DPFL algorithm named DP-FedSAM, which leverages gradient perturbation to mitigate the negative impact of DP.
- [Privacy-Preserving Representations Are Not Enough: Recovering Scene Content From Camera Poses](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Chelani_Privacy-Preserving_Representations_Are_Not_Enough_Recovering_Scene_Content_From_Camera_CVPR_2023_paper.html)

- [CLIP2Protect: Protecting Facial Privacy Using Text-Guided Makeup via Adversarial Latent Search](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Shamshad_CLIP2Protect_Protecting_Facial_Privacy_Using_Text-Guided_Makeup_via_Adversarial_Latent_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “CLIP2Protect: Protecting Facial Privacy Using Text-Guided Makeup via Adversarial Latent Search” investigate?

- [Implicit Identity Leakage: The Stumbling Block to Improving Deepfake Detection Generalization](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Dong_Implicit_Identity_Leakage_The_Stumbling_Block_to_Improving_Deepfake_Detection_CVPR_2023_paper.html)

- [SegLoc: Learning Segmentation-Based Representations for Privacy-Preserving Visual Localization](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Pietrantoni_SegLoc_Learning_Segmentation-Based_Representations_for_Privacy-Preserving_Visual_Localization_CVPR_2023_paper.html)

- [Reinforcement Learning-Based Black-Box Model Inversion Attacks](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Han_Reinforcement_Learning-Based_Black-Box_Model_Inversion_Attacks_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
