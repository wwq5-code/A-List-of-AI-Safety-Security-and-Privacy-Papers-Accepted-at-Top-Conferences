# CVPR 2025

This list focuses on accepted papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems. Papers were screened by title from the [CVPR 2025 official paper list](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/CVPR2025?day=all) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each `Research question` and `What they did` line is one sentence. Method summaries are grounded in the abstract; if no public abstract was available, the entry says so explicitly instead of inferring a contribution from the title.

## 1. LLM and Agent Security

## 2. Backdoors, Poisoning, and Adversarial ML

- [Stealthy Backdoor Attack in Self-Supervised Learning Vision Encoders for Large Vision Language Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Liu_Stealthy_Backdoor_Attack_in_Self-Supervised_Learning_Vision_Encoders_for_Large_CVPR_2025_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Stealthy Backdoor Attack in Self-Supervised Learning Vision Encoders for Large Vision Language Models” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose BadVision, the first method to exploit this vulnerability in SSL vision encoders for LVLMs with novel trigger optimization and backdoor learning techniques.
- [Infighting in the Dark: Multi-Label Backdoor Attack in Federated Learning](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Li_Infighting_in_the_Dark_Multi-Label_Backdoor_Attack_in_Federated_Learning_CVPR_2025_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Infighting in the Dark: Multi-Label Backdoor Attack in Federated Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To address this limitation, the authors propose Mirage, the first non-cooperative MBA strategy in FL that allows attackers to inject effective and persistent backdoors into the global model without collusion by constructing in-distribution (ID) backdoor mapping.
- [Revisiting Backdoor Attacks against Large Vision-Language Models from Domain Shift](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Liang_Revisiting_Backdoor_Attacks_against_Large_Vision-Language_Models_from_Domain_Shift_CVPR_2025_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Revisiting Backdoor Attacks against Large Vision-Language Models from Domain Shift” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce a new evaluation dimension, backdoor domain generalization, to assess attack robustness under visual and text domain shifts.
- [Invisible Backdoor Attack against Self-supervised Learning](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Zhang_Invisible_Backdoor_Attack_against_Self-supervised_Learning_CVPR_2025_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Invisible Backdoor Attack against Self-supervised Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Building on this insight, the authors design an attack using optimized triggers disentangled with the augmented transformation in the SSL, while remaining imperceptible to human vision.
- [Mind the Trojan Horse: Image Prompt Adapter Enabling Scalable and Deceptive Jailbreaking](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Chen_Mind_the_Trojan_Horse_Image_Prompt_Adapter_Enabling_Scalable_and_CVPR_2025_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Mind the Trojan Horse: Image Prompt Adapter Enabling Scalable and Deceptive Jailbreaking” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors demonstrate that, by uploading imperceptible image-space adversarial examples (AEs), the adversary can hijack massive benign users to jailbreak an Image Generation Service (IGS) driven by T2I-IP-DMs and mislead the public to discredit the service provider.
- [Silent Branding Attack: Trigger-free Data Poisoning Attack on Text-to-Image Diffusion Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Jang_Silent_Branding_Attack_Trigger-free_Data_Poisoning_Attack_on_Text-to-Image_Diffusion_CVPR_2025_paper.html)
  - **Research question:** How does “Silent Branding Attack: Trigger-free Data Poisoning Attack on Text-to-Image Diffusion Models” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce the Silent Branding Attack, a novel data poisoning method that manipulates text-to-image diffusion models to generate images containing specific brand logos or symbols without any text triggers.

- [Model Poisoning Attacks to Federated Learning via Multi-Round Consistency](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Xie_Model_Poisoning_Attacks_to_Federated_Learning_via_Multi-Round_Consistency_CVPR_2025_paper.html)

- [Deterministic Certification of Graph Neural Networks against Graph Poisoning Attacks with Arbitrary Perturbations](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Li_Deterministic_Certification_of_Graph_Neural_Networks_against_Graph_Poisoning_Attacks_CVPR_2025_paper.html)

- [Towards Effective and Sparse Adversarial Attack on Spiking Neural Networks via Breaking Invisible Surrogate Gradients](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Lun_Towards_Effective_and_Sparse_Adversarial_Attack_on_Spiking_Neural_Networks_CVPR_2025_paper.html)

- [PSBD: Prediction Shift Uncertainty Unlocks Backdoor Detection](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Li_PSBD_Prediction_Shift_Uncertainty_Unlocks_Backdoor_Detection_CVPR_2025_paper.html)
