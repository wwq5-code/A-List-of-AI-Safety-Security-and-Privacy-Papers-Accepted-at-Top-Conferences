# AAAI 2024

This list focuses on accepted papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems. Papers were screened by title from the [AAAI 2024 official paper list or proceedings](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/issue/view/576) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each `Research question` and `What they did` line is one sentence, and every method summary is grounded in the paper abstract.

## 1. LLM and Agent Security

- [Visual Adversarial Examples Jailbreak Aligned Large Language Models](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i19.30150)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Visual Adversarial Examples Jailbreak Aligned Large Language Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** As an illustration, the authors present a case study in which the authors exploit visual adversarial examples to circumvent the safety guardrail of aligned LLMs with integrated vision.
- [Evaluating AI Red Teaming’s Readiness to Address Environmental Harms: A Thematic Analysis of LLM Discourse](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i21.30542)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Evaluating AI Red Teaming’s Readiness to Address Environmental Harms: A Thematic Analysis of LLM Discourse” investigate?
  - **What they did:** This research explores the discourse surrounding red teaming and aims to identify any themes in the online discussion of potential environmental harms stemming from Large Language Models (LLMs).
- [LLMGuard: Guarding against Unsafe LLM Behavior](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i21.30566)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “LLMGuard: Guarding against Unsafe LLM Behavior” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To alleviate this, the authors present "LLMGuard", a tool that monitors user interactions with an LLM application and flags content against specific behaviours or conversation topics.
- [Image Safeguarding: Reasoning with Conditional Vision Language Model and Obfuscating Unsafe Content Counterfactually](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i2.27835)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Image Safeguarding: Reasoning with Conditional Vision Language Model and Obfuscating Unsafe Content Counterfactually” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors address these key issues by first performing visual reasoning by designing a visual reasoning model (VLM) conditioned on pre-trained unsafe image classifiers to provide an accurate rationale grounded in unsafe image attributes, and then proposing a counterfactual explanation algorithm that minimally identifies and obfuscates unsafe regions for safe viewing, by first utilizing an unsafe image classifier attribution matrix…

## 2. Backdoors, Poisoning, and Adversarial ML

- [Personalization as a Shortcut for Few-Shot Backdoor Attack against Text-to-Image Diffusion Models](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i19.30110)
  - **Research question:** How does “Personalization as a Shortcut for Few-Shot Backdoor Attack against Text-to-Image Diffusion Models” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors investigate a critical and unexplored aspect of text-to-image (T2I) diffusion models - their potential vulnerability to backdoor attacks via personalization.
- [Adv-Diffusion: Imperceptible Adversarial Face Identity Attack via Latent Diffusion Model](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i4.28147)
  - **Research question:** How does “Adv-Diffusion: Imperceptible Adversarial Face Identity Attack via Latent Diffusion Model” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a unified framework Adv-Diffusion that can generate imperceptible adversarial identity perturbations in the latent space but not the raw pixel space, which utilizes strong inpainting capabilities of the latent diffusion model to generate realistic adversarial images.
- [Beyond Traditional Threats: A Persistent Backdoor Attack on Federated Learning](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i19.30131)
  - **Research question:** How does “Beyond Traditional Threats: A Persistent Backdoor Attack on Federated Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Given that research to improve this performance has not been widely noted, the authors propose a Full Combination Backdoor Attack (FCBA) method.
- [BadSAM: Exploring Security Vulnerabilities of SAM via Backdoor Attacks (Student Abstract)](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i21.30448)
  - **Research question:** How does “BadSAM: Exploring Security Vulnerabilities of SAM via Backdoor Attacks (Student Abstract)” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce BadSAM, a backdoor attack tailored for SAM, revealing that customized models can harbor malicious behaviors.
- [A Dual Stealthy Backdoor: From Both Spatial and Frequency Perspectives](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i3.27954)
  - **Research question:** How does “A Dual Stealthy Backdoor: From Both Spatial and Frequency Perspectives” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To address this issue, the authors propose a DUal stealthy BAckdoor attack method named DUBA, which simultaneously considers the invisibility of triggers in both the spatial and frequency domains, to achieve desirable attack performance, while ensuring strong stealthiness.
- [COMBAT: Alternated Training for Effective Clean-Label Backdoor Attacks](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i3.28019)
  - **Research question:** How does “COMBAT: Alternated Training for Effective Clean-Label Backdoor Attacks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a novel mechanism to develop clean-label attacks with outstanding attack performance.
- [BadRL: Sparse Targeted Backdoor Attack against Reinforcement Learning](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i10.29052)
  - **Research question:** How does “BadRL: Sparse Targeted Backdoor Attack against Reinforcement Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a novel approach, BadRL, which focuses on conducting highly sparse backdoor poisoning efforts during training and testing while maintaining successful attacks.
- [DifAttack: Query-Efficient Black-Box Adversarial Attack via Disentangled Feature Space](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i4.28156)
  - **Research question:** How does “DifAttack: Query-Efficient Black-Box Adversarial Attack via Disentangled Feature Space” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors design a novel attack method based on a Disentangled Feature space, called DifAttack, which differs significantly from the existing ones operating over the entire feature space.
- [Progressive Poisoned Data Isolation for Training-Time Backdoor Defense](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i10.29023)
  - **Research question:** How does “Progressive Poisoned Data Isolation for Training-Time Backdoor Defense” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors present a novel and efficacious defense method, termed Progressive Isolation of Poisoned Data (PIPD), that progressively isolates poisoned data to enhance the isolation accuracy and mitigate the risk of benign samples being misclassified as poisoned ones.
- [Enhancing the Robustness of Spiking Neural Networks with Stochastic Gating Mechanisms](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i1.27804)
  - **Research question:** How does “Enhancing the Robustness of Spiking Neural Networks with Stochastic Gating Mechanisms” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Inspired by this, the authors elaborate a stochastic gating spiking neural model for layer-by-layer spike communication, introducing stochasticity to SNNs.
- [Resisting Backdoor Attacks in Federated Learning via Bidirectional Elections and Individual Perspective](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i13.29385)
  - **Research question:** How does “Resisting Backdoor Attacks in Federated Learning via Bidirectional Elections and Individual Perspective” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Unlike previous perspectives from a global view, the authors propose Snowball, a novel anti-backdoor FL framework through bidirectional elections from an individual perspective inspired by one principle deduced by us and two principles in FL and deep learning.
- [Does Few-Shot Learning Suffer from Backdoor Attacks?](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i18.29965)
  - **Research question:** How does “Does Few-Shot Learning Suffer from Backdoor Attacks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** However, in this paper, the authors propose the Few-shot Learning Backdoor Attack (FLBA) to show that FSL can still be vulnerable to backdoor attacks.
- [DataElixir: Purifying Poisoned Dataset to Mitigate Backdoor Attacks via Diffusion Models](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i19.30186)
  - **Research question:** How does “DataElixir: Purifying Poisoned Dataset to Mitigate Backdoor Attacks via Diffusion Models” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose DataElixir, a novel sanitization approach tailored to purify poisoned datasets.
- [Conditional Backdoor Attack via JPEG Compression](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i18.29957)
  - **Research question:** How does “Conditional Backdoor Attack via JPEG Compression” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** In this case, besides the conditional triggering feature, their attack is also stealthy and robust to denoising operations.
- [Mutual-Modality Adversarial Attack with Semantic Perturbation](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i7.28488)
  - **Research question:** How does “Mutual-Modality Adversarial Attack with Semantic Perturbation” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors evaluate their approach on several benchmark datasets and demonstrate that their mutual-modal attack strategy can effectively produce high-transferable attacks, which are stable regardless of the target networks.
- [Step Vulnerability Guided Mean Fluctuation Adversarial Attack against Conditional Diffusion Models](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i7.28503)
  - **Research question:** How does “Step Vulnerability Guided Mean Fluctuation Adversarial Attack against Conditional Diffusion Models” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Utilizing the phenomenon that diffusion models are highly sensitive to the mean value of the input noise, the authors propose the Mean Fluctuation Attack (MFA) to introduce mean fluctuations by shifting the mean values of the estimated noises during the reverse process.
- [Chronic Poisoning: Backdoor Attack against Split Learning](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i15.29591)
  - **Research question:** How does “Chronic Poisoning: Backdoor Attack against Split Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Based on these insights, the authors propose a three-stage backdoor attack framework named SFI.
- [AGS: Affordable and Generalizable Substitute Training for Transferable Adversarial Attack](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i6.28365)
  - **Research question:** How does “AGS: Affordable and Generalizable Substitute Training for Transferable Adversarial Attack” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To tackle the above issues, i.e., high cost and over-fitting on taskspecific models, the authors propose an Affordable and Generalizable Substitute (AGS) training framework tailored for transferbased adversarial attack.
- [LimeAttack: Local Explainable Method for Textual Hard-Label Adversarial Attack](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i17.29950)
  - **Research question:** How does “LimeAttack: Local Explainable Method for Textual Hard-Label Adversarial Attack” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a novel hard-label attack algorithm named LimeAttack, which leverages a local explainable method to approximate word importance ranking, and then adopts beam search to find the optimal solution.
- [SEER: Backdoor Detection for Vision-Language Models through Searching Target Text and Image Trigger Jointly](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i7.28611)
  - **Research question:** How does “SEER: Backdoor Detection for Vision-Language Models through Searching Target Text and Image Trigger Jointly” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose to detect backdoors in vision-language models by jointly searching image triggers and malicious target texts in feature space shared by vision and language modalities.
- [Robust Nonparametric Regression under Poisoning Attack](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i15.29644)
  - **Research question:** How does “Robust Nonparametric Regression under Poisoning Attack” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To address this issue, the authors propose a correction method by projecting the initial estimate to the space of Lipschitz functions.
- [Out of Thin Air: Exploring Data-Free Adversarial Robustness Distillation](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i6.28390)
  - **Research question:** How does “Out of Thin Air: Exploring Data-Free Adversarial Robustness Distillation” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To tackle these issues, the authors propose a challenging but significant task called Data-Free Adversarial Robustness Distillation (DFARD), which aims to train small, easily deployable, robust models without relying on data.
- [Invisible Backdoor Attack against 3D Point Cloud Classifier in Graph Spectral Domain](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i19.30099)
  - **Research question:** How does “Invisible Backdoor Attack against 3D Point Cloud Classifier in Graph Spectral Domain” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Then, the authors design a backdoor training procedure which updates the parameter of backdoor implanting function and victim 3D DNN alternately.
- [Inspecting Prediction Confidence for Detecting Black-Box Backdoor Attacks](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i1.27780)
  - **Research question:** How does “Inspecting Prediction Confidence for Detecting Black-Box Backdoor Attacks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To this end, the authors propose a new defense DTInspector against black-box backdoor attacks, based on a new observation related to the prediction confidence of learning models.
- [Backdoor Adjustment via Group Adaptation for Debiased Coupon Recommendations](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i11.29081)
  - **Research question:** How does “Backdoor Adjustment via Group Adaptation for Debiased Coupon Recommendations” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To address the efficiency challenge, the authors further present a simple but effective dual-tower multi-task framework and leverage the Customized Gate Control (CGC) model architecture, which separately models each user and coupon group with a separate expert module.
- [NeRFail: Neural Radiance Fields-Based Multiview Adversarial Attack](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i19.30113)
  - **Research question:** How does “NeRFail: Neural Radiance Fields-Based Multiview Adversarial Attack” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To do so, the authors propose a transformation mapping from pixels to 3D points such that their attack generates multiview adversarial perturbations by attacking a subset of images with different views, intending to prevent the downstream classifier from correctly predicting images rendered by NeRF from other views.
- [REGLO: Provable Neural Network Repair for Global Robustness Properties](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i11.29094)
  - **Research question:** How does “REGLO: Provable Neural Network Repair for Global Robustness Properties” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors present REGLO, a novel methodology for repairing pretrained neural networks to satisfy global robustness and individual fairness properties.
- [Coupling Graph Neural Networks with Fractional Order Continuous Dynamics: A Robustness Study](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i12.29203)
  - **Research question:** How does “Coupling Graph Neural Networks with Fractional Order Continuous Dynamics: A Robustness Study” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors establish a theoretical foundation outlining the robustness characteristics of graph neural FDE models, highlighting that they maintain more stringent output perturbation bounds in the face of input and graph topology disturbances, compared to their integer-order counterparts.
- [Data Poisoning to Fake a Nash Equilibria for Markov Games](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i14.29529)
  - **Research question:** How does “Data Poisoning to Fake a Nash Equilibria for Markov Games” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors characterize offline data poisoning attacks on Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL), where an attacker may change a data set in an attempt to install a (potentially fictitious) unique Markov-perfect Nash equilibrium for a two-player zero-sum Markov game.
- [Temporal-Distributed Backdoor Attack against Video Based Action Recognition](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i4.28104)
  - **Research question:** How does “Temporal-Distributed Backdoor Attack against Video Based Action Recognition” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce a simple yet effective backdoor attack against video data.

  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Learning to Unlearn: Instance-Wise Unlearning for Pre-trained Classifiers” investigate?

- [UMA: Facilitating Backdoor Scanning via Unlearning-Based Model Ablation](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i19.30183)
