# AAAI 2023

This list focuses on accepted papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems. Papers were screened by title from the [AAAI 2023 official paper list or proceedings](https://aaai.org/proceeding/aaai-37-2023/) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each `Research question` and `What they did` line is one sentence, and every method summary is grounded in the paper abstract.

## 1. Backdoors, Poisoning, and Adversarial ML

- [Imperceptible Adversarial Attack via Invertible Neural Networks](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25115)
  - **Research question:** How does “Imperceptible Adversarial Attack via Invertible Neural Networks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce a novel Adversarial Attack via Invertible Neural Networks (AdvINN) method to produce robust and imperceptible adversarial examples.
- [Neural Architecture Search for Wide Spectrum Adversarial Robustness](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25118)
  - **Research question:** How does “Neural Architecture Search for Wide Spectrum Adversarial Robustness” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** In detail, the authors propose a lightweight Adversarial Noise Estimator to reduce the high cost of generating adversarial noise with respect to different strengths.
- [Defending Backdoor Attacks on Vision Transformer via Patch Processing](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25125)
  - **Research question:** How does “Defending Backdoor Attacks on Vision Transformer via Patch Processing” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors first examine the vulnerability of ViTs against various backdoor attacks and find that ViTs are also quite vulnerable to existing attacks.
- [CSTAR: Towards Compact and Structured Deep Neural Networks with Adversarial Robustness](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25299)
  - **Research question:** How does “CSTAR: Towards Compact and Structured Deep Neural Networks with Adversarial Robustness” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To address this problem, in this paper, the authors propose CSTAR, an efficient solution that simultaneously impose Compactness, high STructuredness and high Adversarial Robustness on the target DNN models.
- [Defending against Backdoor Attacks in Natural Language Generation](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25656)
  - **Research question:** How does “Defending against Backdoor Attacks in Natural Language Generation” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** By giving a formal definition of backdoor attack and defense, the authors investigate this problem on two important NLG tasks, machine translation and dialog generation.
- [Towards Efficient and Domain-Agnostic Evasion Attack with High-Dimensional Categorical Inputs](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25828)
  - **Research question:** How does “Towards Efficient and Domain-Agnostic Evasion Attack with High-Dimensional Categorical Inputs” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** In empirical analysis, the authors compare FEAT with other state-of-the-art domain-agnostic attack methods over various real-world categorical data sets of different applications.
- [Black-Box Adversarial Attack on Time Series Classification](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25896)
  - **Research question:** How does “Black-Box Adversarial Attack on Time Series Classification” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors study a more rigorous black-box setting with attack detection applied, which restricts gradient access and requires the adversarial example to be also stealthy.
- [Federated Robustness Propagation: Sharing Adversarial Robustness in Heterogeneous Federated Learning](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25955)
  - **Research question:** How does “Federated Robustness Propagation: Sharing Adversarial Robustness in Heterogeneous Federated Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors demonstrate the rationality and effectiveness of their method through extensive experiments.
- [Enhancing the Antidote: Improved Pointwise Certifications against Poisoning Attacks](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/26065)
  - **Research question:** How does “Enhancing the Antidote: Improved Pointwise Certifications against Poisoning Attacks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Poisoning attacks can disproportionately influence model behaviour by making small changes to the training corpus.
- [Poisoning with Cerberus: Stealthy and Colluded Backdoor Attack against Federated Learning](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/26083)
  - **Research question:** How does “Poisoning with Cerberus: Stealthy and Colluded Backdoor Attack against Federated Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** In their work, the authors instantiate the attack strategy by proposing a distributed backdoor attack method, namely Cerberus Poisoning (CerP).
- [Training Meta-Surrogate Model for Transferable Adversarial Attack](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/26139)
  - **Research question:** How does “Training Meta-Surrogate Model for Transferable Adversarial Attack” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors show that this goal can be mathematically formulated as a bi-level optimization problem and design a differentiable attacker to make training feasible.
- [Reward Poisoning Attacks on Offline Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/26240)
  - **Research question:** How does “Reward Poisoning Attacks on Offline Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors study reward-poisoning attacks in this setting where an exogenous attacker modifies the rewards in the dataset before the agents see the dataset.
- [Towards Optimal Randomized Strategies in Adversarial Example Game](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/26247)
  - **Research question:** How does “Towards Optimal Randomized Strategies in Adversarial Example Game” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To fill the gap, the authors propose the first algorithm of its kind, called FRAT, which models the problem with a new infinite-dimensional continuous-time flow on probability distribution spaces.
- [DeFL: Defending against Model Poisoning Attacks in Federated Learning via Critical Learning Periods Awareness](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/26271)
  - **Research question:** How does “DeFL: Defending against Model Poisoning Attacks in Federated Learning via Critical Learning Periods Awareness” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Inspired by recent findings on critical learning periods (CLP) in DNNs, where small gradient errors have irrecoverable impact on the final model accuracy, the authors propose a new defense, called a CLP-aware defense against poisoning of FL (DeFL).
- [Delving into the Adversarial Robustness of Federated Learning](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/26331)
  - **Research question:** How does “Delving into the Adversarial Robustness of Federated Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To facilitate a better understanding of the adversarial vulnerability of the existing FL methods, the authors conduct comprehensive robustness evaluations on various attacks and adversarial training methods.
- [On the Vulnerability of Backdoor Defenses for Federated Learning](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/26393)
  - **Research question:** How does “On the Vulnerability of Backdoor Defenses for Federated Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors study whether the current defense mechanisms truly neutralize the backdoor threats from federated learning in a practical setting by proposing a new federated backdoor attack framework for possible countermeasures.
- [SSPAttack: A Simple and Sweet Paradigm for Black-Box Hard-Label Textual Adversarial Attack](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/26553)
  - **Research question:** How does “SSPAttack: A Simple and Sweet Paradigm for Black-Box Hard-Label Textual Adversarial Attack” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a simple and sweet paradigm for hard-label textual adversarial attack, named SSPAttack.

## 2. AI Privacy, Memorization, and Unlearning

- [Pseudo Label-Guided Model Inversion Attack via Conditional Generative Adversarial Network](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25442)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Pseudo Label-Guided Model Inversion Attack via Conditional Generative Adversarial Network” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address these problems, the authors propose Pseudo Label-Guided MI (PLG-MI) attack via conditional GAN (cGAN).
- [Win-Win: A Privacy-Preserving Federated Framework for Dual-Target Cross-Domain Recommendation](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25531)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Win-Win: A Privacy-Preserving Federated Framework for Dual-Target Cross-Domain Recommendation” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address the above challenging problems, the authors propose a novel and unified privacy-preserving federated framework for dual-target CDR, namely P2FCDR.
- [PPGenCDR: A Stable and Robust Framework for Privacy-Preserving Cross-Domain Recommendation](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25566)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “PPGenCDR: A Stable and Robust Framework for Privacy-Preserving Cross-Domain Recommendation” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To fill this gap, the authors propose a privacy-preserving generative cross-domain recommendation (PPGenCDR) framework for PPCDR.
- [Copyright-Certified Distillation Dataset: Distilling One Million Coins into One Bitcoin with Your Private Key](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25794)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Copyright-Certified Distillation Dataset: Distilling One Million Coins into One Bitcoin with Your Private Key” investigate?
  - **What they did:** However, copyright protection for dataset distillation has not been proposed yet, so the authors propose the first method to protect intellectual property by embedding watermarks in the dataset distillation process.
- [Can Bad Teaching Induce Forgetting? Unlearning in Deep Networks Using an Incompetent Teacher](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25879)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Can Bad Teaching Induce Forgetting? Unlearning in Deep Networks Using an Incompetent Teacher” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a novel machine unlearning method by exploring the utility of competent and incompetent teachers in a student-teacher framework to induce forgetfulness.
- [DisGUIDE: Disagreement-Guided Data-Free Model Extraction](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/26150)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “DisGUIDE: Disagreement-Guided Data-Free Model Extraction” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors create a novel approach to get higher accuracy and query efficiency than prior data-free model extraction techniques.
- [Securing Secure Aggregation: Mitigating Multi-Round Privacy Leakage in Federated Learning](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/26177)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Securing Secure Aggregation: Mitigating Multi-Round Privacy Leakage in Federated Learning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address this challenge, the authors introduce a secure aggregation framework, Multi-RoundSecAgg, with multi-round privacy guarantees.
- [Memorization Weights for Instance Reweighting in Adversarial Training](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/26329)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Memorization Weights for Instance Reweighting in Adversarial Training” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a novel algorithm to reweight the training samples based on self-supervised techniques to mitigate the negative effects of the atypical samples.
- [Echo of Neighbors: Privacy Amplification for Personalized Private Federated Learning with Shuffle Model](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/26400)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Echo of Neighbors: Privacy Amplification for Personalized Private Federated Learning with Shuffle Model” investigate?
  - **What they did:** Furthermore, the authors propose a refined framework (S-APES) with the post-sparsification technique to reduce privacy loss in high-dimension scenarios.

## 3. Deepfakes, Watermarking, and Content Provenance

- [Self-Supervised Image Local Forgery Detection by JPEG Compression Trace](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25095)
  - **Research question:** How does “Self-Supervised Image Local Forgery Detection by JPEG Compression Trace” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** The authors firstly analyzed the JPEG compression traces which are mainly caused by different JPEG compression chains, and designed a trace extractor to learn such traces.
- [Self-Supervised Learning for Multilevel Skeleton-Based Forgery Detection via Temporal-Causal Consistency of Actions](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25163)
  - **Research question:** How does “Self-Supervised Learning for Multilevel Skeleton-Based Forgery Detection via Temporal-Causal Consistency of Actions” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** To this end, the authors propose an approach to self-supervised learning of the temporal causality behind human action, which can effectively check TII in skeletal sequences.
- [DENet: Disentangled Embedding Network for Visible Watermark Removal](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25337)
  - **Research question:** How does “DENet: Disentangled Embedding Network for Visible Watermark Removal” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** To this end, inspired by the two-stage coarse-refinement network, the authors propose a novel contrastive learning mechanism to disentangle the high-level embedding semantic information of the images and watermarks, driving the respective network branch more oriented.
- [Flow-Based Robust Watermarking with Invertible Noise Layer for Black-Box Distortions](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25633)
  - **Research question:** How does “Flow-Based Robust Watermarking with Invertible Noise Layer for Black-Box Distortions” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** To address this limitation, this paper proposes a flow-based robust watermarking framework.
- [Deepfake Video Detection via Facial Action Dependencies Estimation](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25658)
  - **Research question:** How does “Deepfake Video Detection via Facial Action Dependencies Estimation” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** The authors formulate the deepfake video detection problem into a graph classification task, and propose a novel paradigm named Facial Action Dependencies Estimation (FADE) for deepfake video detection.
- [Real or Fake Text?: Investigating Human Ability to Detect Boundaries between Human-Written and Machine-Generated Text](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/26501)
  - **Research question:** How does “Real or Fake Text?: Investigating Human Ability to Detect Boundaries between Human-Written and Machine-Generated Text” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** The authors study a more realistic setting where text begins as human-written and transitions to being generated by state-of-the-art neural language models.
- [DeAR: A Deep-Learning-Based Audio Re-recording Resilient Watermarking](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/26550)
  - **Research question:** How does “DeAR: A Deep-Learning-Based Audio Re-recording Resilient Watermarking” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** To address this limitation, this paper proposes DeAR, a deep-learning-based audio re-recording resistant watermarking.

## 4. Other AI Security and Privacy

- [Let Graph Be the Go Board: Gradient-Free Node Injection Attack for Graph Neural Networks via Reinforcement Learning](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25558)
  - **Research question:** What AI security or privacy weakness does “Let Graph Be the Go Board: Gradient-Free Node Injection Attack for Graph Neural Networks via Reinforcement Learning” expose or mitigate?
