# NeurIPS 2024

This list focuses on accepted papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems. Papers were screened by title from the [NeurIPS 2024 official paper list or proceedings](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/papers.html) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each `Research question` and `What they did` line is one sentence, and every method summary is grounded in the paper abstract.

## 1. LLM and Agent Security

- [Robust Prompt Optimization for Defending Language Models Against Jailbreaking Attacks](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/93953)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Robust Prompt Optimization for Defending Language Models Against Jailbreaking Attacks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address this, the authors propose an optimization-based objective for defending LLMs against jailbreaking attacks and an algorithm, Robust Prompt Optimization (RPO), to create robust system-level defenses.
- [SG-Bench: Evaluating LLM Safety Generalization Across Diverse Tasks and Prompt Types](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/97610)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “SG-Bench: Evaluating LLM Safety Generalization Across Diverse Tasks and Prompt Types” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To overcome these issues, the authors developed SG-Bench, a novel benchmark to assess the generalization of LLM safety across various tasks and prompt types.
- [PrivAuditor: Benchmarking Data Protection Vulnerabilities in LLM Adaptation Techniques](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/97662)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “PrivAuditor: Benchmarking Data Protection Vulnerabilities in LLM Adaptation Techniques” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors extensively investigate the privacy vulnerabilities of adapted (fine-tuned) LLMs and benchmark privacy leakage across a wide range of data modalities, state-of-the-art privacy attack methods, adaptation techniques, and model architectures.
- [Improved Few-Shot Jailbreaking Can Circumvent Aligned Language Models and Their Defenses](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/92958)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Improved Few-Shot Jailbreaking Can Circumvent Aligned Language Models and Their Defenses” investigate?
  - **What they did:** In addition, the authors conduct comprehensive and elaborate (e.g., making sure to use correct system prompts) evaluations against other aligned LLMs and advanced defenses, where their method consistently achieves nearly 100% ASRs.

- [CTIBench: A Benchmark for Evaluating LLMs in Cyber Threat Intelligence](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/97556)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “CTIBench: A Benchmark for Evaluating LLMs in Cyber Threat Intelligence” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To bridge this gap, the authors introduce CTIBench, a benchmark designed to assess LLMs' performance in CTI applications.
- [ColJailBreak: Collaborative Generation and Editing for Jailbreaking Text-to-Image Deep Generation](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/94287)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “ColJailBreak: Collaborative Generation and Editing for Jailbreaking Text-to-Image Deep Generation” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose an effective, simple, and difficult-to-detect jailbreaking solution: generating safe content initially with normal text prompts and then editing the generations to embed unsafe content.
- [Bag of Tricks: Benchmarking of Jailbreak Attacks on LLMs](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/97431)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Bag of Tricks: Benchmarking of Jailbreak Attacks on LLMs” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address these issues, the authors introduced JailTrickBench to evaluate the impact of various attack settings on LLM performance and provide a baseline for jailbreak attacks, encouraging the adoption of a standardized evaluation framework.
- [NYU CTF Bench: A Scalable Open-Source Benchmark Dataset for Evaluating LLMs in Offensive Security](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/97547)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “NYU CTF Bench: A Scalable Open-Source Benchmark Dataset for Evaluating LLMs in Offensive Security” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address this, the authors develop a novel method to assess LLMs in solving CTF challenges by creating a scalable, open-source benchmark database specifically designed for these applications.
- [AgentDojo: A Dynamic Environment to Evaluate Prompt Injection Attacks and Defenses for LLM Agents](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/97522)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “AgentDojo: A Dynamic Environment to Evaluate Prompt Injection Attacks and Defenses for LLM Agents” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To measure the adversarial robustness of AI agents, the authors introduce AgentDojo, an evaluation framework for agents that execute tools over untrusted data.
- [WildGuard: Open One-stop Moderation Tools for Safety Risks, Jailbreaks, and Refusals of LLMs](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/97764)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “WildGuard: Open One-stop Moderation Tools for Safety Risks, Jailbreaks, and Refusals of LLMs” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address these challenges, the authors construct WildGuardMix, a large-scale and carefully balanced multi-task safety moderation dataset with 92K labeled examples that cover vanilla (direct) prompts and adversarial jailbreaks, paired with various refusal and compliance responses.
- [Mission Impossible: A Statistical Perspective on Jailbreaking LLMs](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/94247)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Mission Impossible: A Statistical Perspective on Jailbreaking LLMs” investigate?
  - **What they did:** Under their framework, the authors first show that pretrained LLMs will mimic harmful behaviour if present in the training corpus.
- [Fight Back Against Jailbreaking via Prompt Adversarial Tuning](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/93692)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Fight Back Against Jailbreaking via Prompt Adversarial Tuning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** Motivated by adversarial training paradigms for achieving reliable robustness, the authors propose an approach named Prompt Adversarial Tuning (PAT) that trains a prompt control attached to the user prompt as a guard prefix.
- [Refusal in Language Models Is Mediated by a Single Direction](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/93566)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Refusal in Language Models Is Mediated by a Single Direction” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors show that refusal is mediated by a one-dimensional subspace, across 13 popular open-source chat models up to 72B parameters in size.
- [Jailbreaking Large Language Models Against Moderation Guardrails via Cipher Characters](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/96243)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Jailbreaking Large Language Models Against Moderation Guardrails via Cipher Characters” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address this issue, the authors introduce JAMBench, a harmful behavior benchmark designed to trigger and evaluate moderation guardrails.
- [Tree of Attacks: Jailbreaking Black-Box LLMs Automatically](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/95078)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Tree of Attacks: Jailbreaking Black-Box LLMs Automatically” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors present Tree of Attacks with Pruning (TAP), an automated method for generating jailbreaks that only requires black-box access to the target LLM.
- [When LLM Meets DRL: Advancing Jailbreaking Efficiency via DRL-guided Search](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/95953)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “When LLM Meets DRL: Advancing Jailbreaking Efficiency via DRL-guided Search” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose RLbreaker, a black-box jailbreaking attack driven by deep reinforcement learning (DRL).
- [On scalable oversight with weak LLMs judging strong LLMs](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/95397)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “On scalable oversight with weak LLMs judging strong LLMs” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors study debate, where two AI's compete to convince a judge; consultancy, where a single AI tries to convince a judge that asks questions;and compare to a baseline of direct question-answering, where the judge just answers outright without the AI.
- [Rule Based Rewards for Language Model Safety](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/95232)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Rule Based Rewards for Language Model Safety” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a novel preference modeling approach that utilizes AI feedback and only requires a small amount of human data.
- [Lisa: Lazy Safety Alignment for Large Language Models against Harmful Fine-tuning Attack](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/95174)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Lisa: Lazy Safety Alignment for Large Language Models against Harmful Fine-tuning Attack” investigate?
  - **What they did:** For the first time in the literature, the authors show that the jail-break effect can be mitigated by separating two states in the fine-tuning stage to respectively optimize over the alignment and user datasets.
- [MoGU: A Framework for Enhancing Safety of LLMs While Preserving Their Usability](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/95073)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “MoGU: A Framework for Enhancing Safety of LLMs While Preserving Their Usability” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To solve this problem, the authors introduce the MoGU framework, designed to enhance LLMs' safety while preserving their usability.
- [Many-shot Jailbreaking](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/94370)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Many-shot Jailbreaking” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors investigate a family of simple long-context attacks on large language models: prompting with hundreds of demonstrations of undesirable behavior.
- [One-Shot Safety Alignment for Large Language Models via Optimal Dualization](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/94361)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “One-Shot Safety Alignment for Large Language Models via Optimal Dualization” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors present a perspective of dualization that reduces constrained alignment to an equivalent unconstrained alignment problem.
- [Gradient Cuff: Detecting Jailbreak Attacks on Large Language Models by Exploring Refusal Loss Landscapes](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/93211)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Gradient Cuff: Detecting Jailbreak Attacks on Large Language Models by Exploring Refusal Loss Landscapes” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address this challenge, this paper defines and investigates the Refusal Loss of LLMs and then proposes a method called Gradient Cuff to detect jailbreak attempts.
- [Vaccine: Perturbation-aware Alignment for Large Language Models against Harmful Fine-tuning Attack](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/93799)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Vaccine: Perturbation-aware Alignment for Large Language Models against Harmful Fine-tuning Attack” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors conduct an empirical analysis and uncovera harmful embedding drift phenomenon, showing a probable cause of the alignment-broken effect.
- [Efficient LLM Jailbreak via Adaptive Dense-to-sparse Constrained Optimization](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/94489)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Efficient LLM Jailbreak via Adaptive Dense-to-sparse Constrained Optimization” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce a novel token-level attack method, Adaptive Dense-to-Sparse Constrained Optimization (ADC), which has been shown to successfully jailbreak multiple open-source LLMs.
- [Improved Generation of Adversarial Examples Against Safety-aligned LLMs](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/96362)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Improved Generation of Adversarial Examples Against Safety-aligned LLMs” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors explore a new perspective on this problem, suggesting that it can be alleviated by leveraging innovations inspired in transfer-based attacks that were originally proposed for attacking black-box image classification models.
- [Efficient Adversarial Training in LLMs with Continuous Attacks](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/96357)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Efficient Adversarial Training in LLMs with Continuous Attacks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a fast adversarial training algorithm (C-AdvUL) composed of two losses: the first makes the model robust on continuous embedding attacks computed on an adversarial behaviour dataset; the second ensures the usefulness of the final model by fine-tuning on utility data.

## 2. Backdoors, Poisoning, and Adversarial ML

## 3. AI Privacy, Memorization, and Unlearning

- [WAGLE: Strategic Weight Attribution for Effective and Modular Unlearning in Large Language Models](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/94863)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “WAGLE: Strategic Weight Attribution for Effective and Modular Unlearning in Large Language Models” investigate?
