# ICML 2025

This list focuses on accepted papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems. Papers were screened by title from the [ICML 2025 official paper list](https://icml.cc/virtual/2025/papers.html) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each `Research question` and `What they did` line is one sentence. Method summaries are grounded in the abstract; if no public abstract was available, the entry says so explicitly instead of inferring a contribution from the title.

  - **What they did:** To tackle these challenges, in this paper, the authors study the problem of adversarial attack and adversarial robustness w.r.t. two terms: fairness and accuracy.
