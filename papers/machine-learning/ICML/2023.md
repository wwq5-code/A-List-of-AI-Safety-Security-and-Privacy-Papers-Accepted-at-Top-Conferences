# ICML 2023

This list focuses on accepted papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems. Papers were screened by title from the [ICML 2023 official paper list or proceedings](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/papers.html) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each `Research question` and `What they did` line is one sentence, and every method summary is grounded in the paper abstract.

## 1. Backdoors, Poisoning, and Adversarial ML

- [Poisoning Language Models During Instruction Tuning](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24997)
  - **Research question:** How does “Poisoning Language Models During Instruction Tuning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors evaluate their method on open-source instruction-tuned LMs.
- [Adversarial robustness of amortized Bayesian inference](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/23637)
  - **Research question:** How does “Adversarial robustness of amortized Bayesian inference” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Here, the authors study the adversarial robustness of amortized Bayesian inference, focusing on simulation-based estimation of multi-dimensional posterior distributions.
- [Exploring Model Dynamics for Accumulative Poisoning Discovery](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/25122)
  - **Research question:** How does “Exploring Model Dynamics for Accumulative Poisoning Discovery” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors dive into the perspective of model dynamics and propose a novel information measure, namely, Memorization Discrepancy, to explore the defense via the model-level information.
- [Stratified Adversarial Robustness with Rejection](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/25097)
  - **Research question:** How does “Stratified Adversarial Robustness with Rejection” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors study adversarially-robust classification with rejection in the stratified rejection setting, where the rejection cost is modeled by rejection loss functions monotonically non-increasing in the perturbation magnitude.
- [Adversarial Example Does Good: Preventing Painting Imitation from Diffusion Models via Adversarial Examples](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/23956)
  - **Research question:** How does “Adversarial Example Does Good: Preventing Painting Imitation from Diffusion Models via Adversarial Examples” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Specifically, the authors first build a theoretical framework to define and evaluate the adversarial examples for DMs.
- [UMD: Unsupervised Model Detection for X2X Backdoor Attacks](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24572)
  - **Research question:** How does “UMD: Unsupervised Model Detection for X2X Backdoor Attacks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose UMD, the first Unsupervised Model Detection method that effectively detects X2X backdoor attacks via a joint inference of the adversarial (source, target) class pairs.
- [LeadFL: Client Self-Defense against Model Poisoning in Federated Learning](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24161)
  - **Research question:** How does “LeadFL: Client Self-Defense against Model Poisoning in Federated Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a client-self defense, LeadFL, that is combined with existing server-side defenses to thwart backdoor and targeted attacks.
- [Data Poisoning Attacks Against Multimodal Encoders](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24040)
  - **Research question:** How does “Data Poisoning Attacks Against Multimodal Encoders” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To answer the two questions, the authors propose three types of poisoning attacks against multimodal models.
- [Identification of the Adversary from a Single Adversarial Example](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/23831)
  - **Research question:** How does “Identification of the Adversary from a Single Adversarial Example” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To make the tracing viable, the authors design a random mask watermarking mechanism to differentiate adversarial examples from different copies.
- [Improving Adversarial Robustness by Putting More Regularizations on Less Robust Samples](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24095)
  - **Research question:** How does “Improving Adversarial Robustness by Putting More Regularizations on Less Robust Samples” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Theoretically, the authors show that their algorithm can be understood as an algorithm of minimizing a newly derived upper bound of the robust risk.
- [Rethinking Backdoor Attacks](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/23636)
  - **Research question:** How does “Rethinking Backdoor Attacks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors present a different approach to the backdoor attack problem.
- [Improving Adversarial Robustness Through the Contrastive-Guided Diffusion Process](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24156)
  - **Research question:** How does “Improving Adversarial Robustness Through the Contrastive-Guided Diffusion Process” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors first analyze the optimality condition of synthetic distribution for achieving improved robust accuracy.
- [Improving Adversarial Robustness of Deep Equilibrium Models with Explicit Regulations Along the Neural Dynamics](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24207)
  - **Research question:** How does “Improving Adversarial Robustness of Deep Equilibrium Models with Explicit Regulations Along the Neural Dynamics” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Informed by the correlation between the entropy of dynamical systems and their stability properties, the authors propose reducing prediction entropy by progressively updating inputs along the neural dynamics.
- [A Critical Revisit of Adversarial Robustness in 3D Point Cloud Recognition with Diffusion-Driven Purification](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24798)
  - **Research question:** How does “A Critical Revisit of Adversarial Robustness in 3D Point Cloud Recognition with Diffusion-Driven Purification” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To bridge the gap, the authors propose PointDP, a purification strategy that leverages diffusion models to defend against 3D adversarial attacks.
- [Probabilistic Categorical Adversarial Attack and Adversarial Training](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24513)
  - **Research question:** How does “Probabilistic Categorical Adversarial Attack and Adversarial Training” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To bridge this gap, the authors propose a novel framework, Probabilistic Categorical Adversarial Attack (or PCAA).
- [BNN-DP: Robustness Certification of Bayesian Neural Networks via Dynamic Programming](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/23788)
  - **Research question:** How does “BNN-DP: Robustness Certification of Bayesian Neural Networks via Dynamic Programming” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce BNN-DP, an efficient algorithmic framework for analysis of adversarial robustness of Bayesian Neural Networks (BNNs).
- [Poisoning Generative Replay in Continual Learning to Promote Forgetting](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24395)
  - **Research question:** How does “Poisoning Generative Replay in Continual Learning to Promote Forgetting” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors investigate this issue in the context of continual learning, where generative replayers are utilized to tackle catastrophic forgetting.
- [Exploring the Limits of Model-Targeted Indiscriminate Data Poisoning Attacks](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24007)
  - **Research question:** How does “Exploring the Limits of Model-Targeted Indiscriminate Data Poisoning Attacks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce the notion of model poisoning reachability as a technical tool to explore the intrinsic limits of data poisoning attacks towards target parameters (i.e., model-targeted attacks).
- [Phase-aware Adversarial Defense for Improving Adversarial Robustness](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24162)
  - **Research question:** How does “Phase-aware Adversarial Defense for Improving Adversarial Robustness” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Inspired by the cognitive science, the authors investigate the interference of adversarial noise from the perspective of image phase, and find ordinarily-trained models lack enough robustness against phase-level perturbations.
- [Graph Contrastive Backdoor Attacks](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24439)
  - **Research question:** How does “Graph Contrastive Backdoor Attacks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors formulate their attacks as optimization problems and solve them with a novel discrete optimization technique to overcome the discrete nature of graph-structured data.
- [Run-off Election: Improved Provable Defense against Data Poisoning Attacks](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24468)
  - **Research question:** How does “Run-off Election: Improved Provable Defense against Data Poisoning Attacks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors evaluate their methods on MNIST, CIFAR-10, and GTSRB and obtain improvements in certified accuracy by up to $3\%$-$4\%$.
- [Understanding the Impact of Adversarial Robustness on Accuracy Disparity](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24915)
  - **Research question:** How does “Understanding the Impact of Adversarial Robustness on Accuracy Disparity” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Furthermore, the authors also show that such effects extend beyond the Gaussian mixture model, by generalizing their data model to the general family of stable distributions.
- [Reconstructive Neuron Pruning for Backdoor Defense](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24692)
  - **Research question:** How does “Reconstructive Neuron Pruning for Backdoor Defense” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a novel defense called Reconstructive Neuron Pruning (RNP) to expose and prune backdoor neurons via an unlearning and then recovering process.
- [Revisiting Data-Free Knowledge Distillation with Poisoned Teachers](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/23792)
  - **Research question:** How does “Revisiting Data-Free Knowledge Distillation with Poisoned Teachers” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors make the first effort to uncover the security risk of data-free KD w.r.t. untrusted pre-trained models.
- [Understanding Backdoor Attacks through the Adaptability Hypothesis](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/25104)
  - **Research question:** How does “Understanding Backdoor Attacks through the Adaptability Hypothesis” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose the adaptability hypothesis to understand when and why a backdoor attack works for general learning models, including deep neural networks, based on the theoretical investigation of classical kernel-based learning models.
- [Adversarial Parameter Attack on Deep Neural Networks](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24781)
  - **Research question:** How does “Adversarial Parameter Attack on Deep Neural Networks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To make the attack more stealthy, in this paper, the adversarial parameter attack is proposed, in which small perturbations to the parameters of the network are made such that the accuracy of the attacked network does not decrease much, but its robustness against adversarial example attacks becomes much lower.

## 2. AI Privacy, Memorization, and Unlearning

- [Fast Federated Machine Unlearning with Nonlinear Functional Theory](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/23542)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Fast Federated Machine Unlearning with Nonlinear Functional Theory” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose to employ nonlinear functional analysis techniques to refine the local MU models as output functions of a Nemytskii operator.
- [Differential Privacy has Bounded Impact on Fairness in Classification](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24848)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Differential Privacy has Bounded Impact on Fairness in Classification” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors theoretically study the impact of differential privacy on fairness in classification.
- [Privacy-Aware Compression for Federated Learning Through Numerical Mechanism Design](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24557)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Privacy-Aware Compression for Federated Learning Through Numerical Mechanism Design” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors build upon it by introducing a new interpolation procedure in the numerical design process that allows for a far more efficient privacy analysis.
- [From Noisy Fixed-Point Iterations to Private ADMM for Centralized and Federated Learning](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24845)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “From Noisy Fixed-Point Iterations to Private ADMM for Centralized and Federated Learning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors study differentially private (DP) machine learning algorithms as instances of noisy fixed-point iterations, in order to derive privacy and utility results from this well-studied framework.
- [Bag of Tricks for Training Data Extraction from Language Models](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24166)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Bag of Tricks for Training Data Extraction from Language Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors investigate and benchmark tricks for improving training data extraction using a publicly available dataset.
- [Multi-Task Differential Privacy Under Distribution Skew](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/23724)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Multi-Task Differential Privacy Under Distribution Skew” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors study the problem of multi-task learning under user-level differential privacy, in which n users contribute data to m tasks, each involving a subset of users.
- [Effectively Using Public Data in Privacy Preserving Machine Learning](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24245)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Effectively Using Public Data in Privacy Preserving Machine Learning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** Specifically, the authors introduce DOPE-SGD, a modified DP-SGD algorithm that leverages public data during its training.
- [TabLeak: Tabular Data Leakage in Federated Learning](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/23499)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “TabLeak: Tabular Data Leakage in Federated Learning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors evaluate TabLeak on four tabular datasets for both FedSGD and FedAvg training protocols, and show that it successfully breaks several settings previously deemed safe.
- [Forget Unlearning: Towards True Data-Deletion in Machine Learning](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/23753)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Forget Unlearning: Towards True Data-Deletion in Machine Learning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address these, the authors propose a sound deletion guarantee and show that ensuring the privacy of existing records is necessary for the privacy of deleted records.
- [From Adaptive Query Release to Machine Unlearning](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/23690)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “From Adaptive Query Release to Machine Unlearning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors formalize the problem of machine unlearning as design of efficient unlearning algorithms corresponding to learning algorithms which perform a selection of adaptive queries from structured query classes.
- [GuardHFL: Privacy Guardian for Heterogeneous Federated Learning](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24004)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “GuardHFL: Privacy Guardian for Heterogeneous Federated Learning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** Heterogeneous federated learning (HFL) enables clients with different computation and communication capabilities to collaboratively train their own customized models via a query-response paradigm on auxiliary datasets.
- [Federated Linear Contextual Bandits with User-level Differential Privacy](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24096)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Federated Linear Contextual Bandits with User-level Differential Privacy” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors first introduce a unified federated bandits framework that can accommodate various definitions of DP in the sequential decision-making setting.
- [Differential Privacy, Linguistic Fairness, and Training Data Influence: Impossibility and Possibility Theorems for Multilingual Language Models](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/23826)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Differential Privacy, Linguistic Fairness, and Training Data Influence: Impossibility and Possibility Theorems for Multilingual Language Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors show that multilingual compression and linguistic fairness are compatible with differential privacy, but that differential privacy is at odds with training data influence sparsity, an objective for transparency.
- [From Robustness to Privacy and Back](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24344)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “From Robustness to Privacy and Back” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors study the relationship between two desiderata of algorithms in statistical inference and machine learning---differential privacy and robustness to adversarial data corruptions.
- [Private Federated Learning with Autotuned Compression](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/23509)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Private Federated Learning with Autotuned Compression” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors demonstrate the effectiveness of their approach on real-world datasets by achieving favorable compression rates without the need for tuning.
- [Analyzing Privacy Leakage in Machine Learning via Multiple Hypothesis Testing: A Lesson From Fano](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24919)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Analyzing Privacy Leakage in Machine Learning via Multiple Hypothesis Testing: A Lesson From Fano” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors study data reconstruction attacks for discrete data and analyze it under the framework of multiple hypothesis testing.
- [Cocktail Party Attack: Breaking Aggregation-Based Privacy in Federated Learning Using Independent Component Analysis](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24639)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Cocktail Party Attack: Breaking Aggregation-Based Privacy in Federated Learning Using Independent Component Analysis” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose the Cocktail Party Attack (CPA) that, contrary to prior belief, is able to recover the private inputs from gradients/weight updates aggregated over as many as 1024 samples.
- [Bayesian Estimation of Differential Privacy](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/23440)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Bayesian Estimation of Differential Privacy” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors implement an end-to-end system for privacy estimation that integrates their approach and state-of-the-art membership inference attacks, and evaluate it on text and vision classification tasks.
- [On the Privacy-Robustness-Utility Trilemma in Distributed Learning](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24614)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “On the Privacy-Robustness-Utility Trilemma in Distributed Learning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors present the first tight analysis of the error incurred by any algorithm ensuring robustness against a fraction of adversarial machines, as well as differential privacy (DP) for honest machines' data against any other curious entity.
- [Deep Regression Unlearning](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24434)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Deep Regression Unlearning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce deep regression unlearning methods that generalize well and are robust to privacy attacks.
- [Label differential privacy and private training data release](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/23692)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Label differential privacy and private training data release” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors study differentially private mechanisms for sharing training data in machine learning settings.
- [Multi-Epoch Matrix Factorization Mechanisms for Private Machine Learning](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/25003)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Multi-Epoch Matrix Factorization Mechanisms for Private Machine Learning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce new differentially private (DP) mechanisms for gradient-based machine learning (ML) with multiple passes (epochs) over a dataset, substantially improving the achievable privacy-utility-computation tradeoffs.
- [FedVS: Straggler-Resilient and Privacy-Preserving Vertical Federated Learning for Split Models](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24392)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “FedVS: Straggler-Resilient and Privacy-Preserving Vertical Federated Learning for Split Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose FedVS to simultaneously address these two challenges.
- [Demystifying Uneven Vulnerability of Link Stealing Attacks against Graph Neural Networks](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24543)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Demystifying Uneven Vulnerability of Link Stealing Attacks against Graph Neural Networks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors first present theoretical evidence of the uneven vulnerability of GNNs to link stealing attacks, which lays the foundation for demystifying such uneven risks among different groups of edges.
- [HETAL: Efficient Privacy-preserving Transfer Learning with Homomorphic Encryption](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24180)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “HETAL: Efficient Privacy-preserving Transfer Learning with Homomorphic Encryption” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors present HETAL, an efficient Homomorphic Encryption based Transfer Learning algorithm, that protects the client's privacy in training tasks by encrypting the client data using the CKKS homomorphic encryption scheme.
- [Are Diffusion Models Vulnerable to Membership Inference Attacks?](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24657)
  - **Research question:** What privacy, memorization, extraction, or forgetting problem does “Are Diffusion Models Vulnerable to Membership Inference Attacks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address this gap, the authors propose Step-wise Error Comparing Membership Inference (SecMI), a query-based MIA that infers memberships by assessing the matching of forward process posterior estimation at each timestep.

## 3. Secure and Verifiable AI Systems

- [SRATTA: Sample Re-ATTribution Attack of Secure Aggregation in Federated Learning.](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24896)
  - **Research question:** How does “SRATTA: Sample Re-ATTribution Attack of Secure Aggregation in Federated Learning” make AI training or inference more secure, private, or verifiable?
  - **What they did:** The authors present SRATTA an attack relying only on aggregated models which, under realistic assumptions, (i) recovers data samples from the different clients, and (ii) groups data samples coming from the same client together.

## 4. Deepfakes, Watermarking, and Content Provenance

- [Margin-based Neural Network Watermarking](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/25193)
  - **Research question:** How does “Margin-based Neural Network Watermarking” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?

- [A Watermark for Large Language Models](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24544)
  - **Research question:** How does “A Watermark for Large Language Models” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?

- [Protecting Language Generation Models via Invisible Watermarking](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24208)

- [Do You Remember? Overcoming Catastrophic Forgetting for Fake Audio Detection](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24753)
  - **Research question:** How does “Do You Remember? Overcoming Catastrophic Forgetting for Fake Audio Detection” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?
  - **What they did:** The authors also evaluate their approach across multiple datasets and obtain a significant performance improvement on cross-dataset experiments.
- [DetectGPT: Zero-Shot Machine-Generated Text Detection using Probability Curvature](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24553)
  - **Research question:** How does “DetectGPT: Zero-Shot Machine-Generated Text Detection using Probability Curvature” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?

- [On Provable Copyright Protection for Generative Models](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/23825)
  - **Research question:** How does “On Provable Copyright Protection for Generative Models” address generated-content detection, attribution, authenticity, copyright, or provenance?

## 5. AI Safety, Alignment, and Responsible AI

- [How to Trust Your Diffusion Model: A Convex Optimization Approach to Conformal Risk Control](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24442)
  - **Research question:** How does “How to Trust Your Diffusion Model: A Convex Optimization Approach to Conformal Risk Control” evaluate or improve AI safety, alignment, oversight, or responsible deployment?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose K-RCPS, a convex-optimization extension of conformal risk control that gives diffusion-model outputs calibrated pixelwise intervals while controlling risk and minimizing interval length.

## 6. Other AI Security and Privacy

- [SpENCNN: Orchestrating Encoding and Sparsity for Fast Homomorphically Encrypted Neural Network Inference](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24295)
  - **Research question:** What AI security or privacy weakness does “SpENCNN: Orchestrating Encoding and Sparsity for Fast Homomorphically Encrypted Neural Network Inference” expose or mitigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a HE-based fast neural network (NN) inference framework--SpENCNN built upon the co-design of HE operation-aware model sparsity and the single-instruction-multiple-data (SIMD)-friendly data packing, to improve NN inference latency.
- [Preprocessors Matter! Realistic Decision-Based Attacks on Machine Learning Systems](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/24809)
  - **Research question:** What AI security or privacy weakness does “Preprocessors Matter! Realistic Decision-Based Attacks on Machine Learning Systems” expose or mitigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors find that by adding a single preprocessor in front of a classifier, state-of-the-art query-based attacks are up to seven× less effective at attacking a prediction pipeline than at attacking the model alone.
- [Surrogate Model Extension (SME): A Fast and Accurate Weight Update Attack on Federated Learning](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/25005)
  - **Research question:** What AI security or privacy weakness does “Surrogate Model Extension (SME): A Fast and Accurate Weight Update Attack on Federated Learning” expose or mitigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a principled way to extend gradient inversion attacks to weight updates in FL, thereby better exposing weaknesses in the presumed privacy protection inherent in FL.
- [Towards Bridging the Gaps between the Right to Explanation and the Right to be Forgotten](https://icml.cc/virtual/2023/poster/23726)
  - **Research question:** What AI security or privacy weakness does “Towards Bridging the Gaps between the Right to Explanation and the Right to be Forgotten” expose or mitigate?
  - **What they did:** To this end, the authors formulate a novel optimization problem to generate explanations that are robust to model updates due to the removal of training data instances by data deletion requests.
