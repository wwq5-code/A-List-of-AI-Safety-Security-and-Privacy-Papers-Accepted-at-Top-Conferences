# ICLR 2025

This list focuses on accepted papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems. Papers were screened by title from the [ICLR 2025 official paper list](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/papers.html) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each `Research question` and `What they did` line is one sentence. Method summaries are grounded in the abstract; if no public abstract was available, the entry says so explicitly instead of inferring a contribution from the title.

## 1. LLM and Agent Security

- [SORRY-Bench: Systematically Evaluating Large Language Model Safety Refusal](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/29252)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “SORRY-Bench: Systematically Evaluating Large Language Model Safety Refusal” investigate?
  - **What they did:** For example, among the ten existing datasets that the authors evaluated, tests for refusals of self-harm instructions are over 3x less represented than tests for fraudulent activities.
- [AutoDAN-Turbo: A Lifelong Agent for Strategy Self-Exploration to Jailbreak LLMs](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/29096)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “AutoDAN-Turbo: A Lifelong Agent for Strategy Self-Exploration to Jailbreak LLMs” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose AutoDAN-Turbo, a black-box jailbreak method that can automatically discover as many jailbreak strategies as possible from scratch, without any human intervention or predefined scopes (e.g., specified candidate strategies), and use them for red-teaming.
- [Functional Homotopy: Smoothing Discrete Optimization via Continuous Parameters for LLM Jailbreak Attacks](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/27963)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Functional Homotopy: Smoothing Discrete Optimization via Continuous Parameters for LLM Jailbreak Attacks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce a novel optimization approach, termed the functional homotopy method, which leverages the functional duality between model training and input generation.
- [BingoGuard: LLM Content Moderation Tools with Risk Levels](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/30227)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “BingoGuard: LLM Content Moderation Tools with Risk Levels” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address the lack of annotations on levels of severity, the authors propose a scalable generate-then-filter framework that first generates responses across different severity levels and then filters out low-quality responses.
- [$R^2$-Guard: Robust Reasoning Enabled LLM Guardrail via Knowledge-Enhanced Logical Reasoning](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/30497)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “$R^2$-Guard: Robust Reasoning Enabled LLM Guardrail via Knowledge-Enhanced Logical Reasoning” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To address these limitations, the authors propose $R^2$-Guard, a robust reasoning enabled LLM guardrail via knowledge-enhanced logical reasoning.
- [AdvWave: Stealthy Adversarial Jailbreak Attack against Large Audio-Language Models](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/31267)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “AdvWave: Stealthy Adversarial Jailbreak Attack against Large Audio-Language Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To overcome these challenges, the authors develop AdvWave, the first jailbreak framework against LALMs.
- [Booster: Tackling Harmful Fine-tuning for Large Language Models via Attenuating Harmful Perturbation](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/28050)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Booster: Tackling Harmful Fine-tuning for Large Language Models via Attenuating Harmful Perturbation” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To this end, the authors in this paper show that \textit{harmful perturbation} over the model weights could be a probable cause of alignment-broken.
- [Durable Quantization Conditioned Misalignment Attack on Large Language Models](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/31042)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Durable Quantization Conditioned Misalignment Attack on Large Language Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce the Quantization Conditioned Misalignment (Q-Misalign) attack, a novel threat in which safety misalignment remains dormant in a full-precision LLM but becomes exploitable post-quantization.
- [Programming Refusal with Conditional Activation Steering](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/29806)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Programming Refusal with Conditional Activation Steering” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose Conditional Activation Steering (CAST), which analyzes LLM activation patterns during inference to selectively apply or withhold activation steering based on the input context.
- [Scaling Laws for Adversarial Attacks on Language Model Activations and Tokens](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/29238)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Scaling Laws for Adversarial Attacks on Language Model Activations and Tokens” investigate?
  - **What they did:** By manipulating a relatively small subset of model activations, $a$, the authors demonstrate the ability to control the exact prediction of a significant number (in some cases up to 1000) of subsequent tokens $t$.
- [Understanding and Enhancing the Transferability of Jailbreaking Attacks](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/29142)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Understanding and Enhancing the Transferability of Jailbreaking Attacks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To this end, the authors propose the Perceived-importance Flatten (PiF) method, which uniformly disperses the model's focus across neutral-intent tokens in the original input, thus obscuring malicious-intent tokens without relying on overfitted adversarial sequences.
- [Beyond Mere Token Analysis: A Hypergraph Metric Space Framework for Defending Against Socially Engineered LLM Attacks](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/28172)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Beyond Mere Token Analysis: A Hypergraph Metric Space Framework for Defending Against Socially Engineered LLM Attacks” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To combat such threats, the authors propose a promising approach to enhancing LLM safety by leveraging the underlying geometry of input prompt token embeddings using hypergraphs.

## 2. Backdoors, Poisoning, and Adversarial ML

- [Improved Diffusion-based Generative Model with Better Adversarial Robustness](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/31218)
  - **Research question:** How does “Improved Diffusion-based Generative Model with Better Adversarial Robustness” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To obviate this, the authors analyze the training objective of DPMs and theoretically demonstrate that this mismatch can be alleviated through Distributionally Robust Optimization (DRO), which is equivalent to performing robustness-driven Adversarial Training (AT) on DPMs.
- [Robustness Inspired Graph Backdoor Defense](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/28019)

- [Bad-PFL: Exploiting Backdoor Attacks against Personalized Federated Learning](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/30844)
  - **Research question:** How does “Bad-PFL: Exploiting Backdoor Attacks against Personalized Federated Learning” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors design Bad-PFL, which uses mutually reinforced features from natural data as durable triggers that survive personalization, and evaluate it across three datasets, multiple PFL methods, and defenses.
