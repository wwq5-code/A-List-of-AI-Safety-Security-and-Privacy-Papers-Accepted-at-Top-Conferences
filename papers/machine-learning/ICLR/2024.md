# ICLR 2024

This list focuses on accepted papers about the security, privacy, safety, and trustworthiness of AI/ML systems. Papers were screened by title from the [ICLR 2024 official paper list or proceedings](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/papers.html) and then checked against their publicly available abstracts.

Each `Research question` and `What they did` line is one sentence, and every method summary is grounded in the paper abstract.

## 1. LLM and Agent Security

- [Tensor Trust: Interpretable Prompt Injection Attacks from an Online Game](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/18168)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Tensor Trust: Interpretable Prompt Injection Attacks from an Online Game” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To help researchers study this problem, the authors present a dataset of over 563,000 prompt injection attacks and 118,000 prompt-based "defenses" against prompt injection, all created by players of an online game called Tensor Trust.
- [AutoDAN: Generating Stealthy Jailbreak Prompts on Aligned Large Language Models](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/19366)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “AutoDAN: Generating Stealthy Jailbreak Prompts on Aligned Large Language Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce AutoDAN, a novel jailbreak attack against aligned LLMs.
- [Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language Models](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/17593)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors investigate the prevalence of sycophancy in RLHF-trained models and whether human preference judgments are responsible.
- [Multilingual Jailbreak Challenges in Large Language Models](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/17543)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Multilingual Jailbreak Challenges in Large Language Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** To handle such a challenge in the multilingual context, the authors propose a novel \textsc{Self-Defense} framework that automatically generates multilingual training data for safety fine-tuning.
- [Jailbreak in pieces: Compositional Adversarial Attacks on Multi-Modal Language Models](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/17767)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Jailbreak in pieces: Compositional Adversarial Attacks on Multi-Modal Language Models” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce new jailbreak attacks on vision language models (VLMs), which use aligned LLMs and are resilient to text-only jailbreak attacks.
- [An LLM can Fool Itself: A Prompt-Based Adversarial Attack](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/18503)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “An LLM can Fool Itself: A Prompt-Based Adversarial Attack” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose an efficient tool to audit the LLM’s adversarial robustness via a prompt-based adversarial attack (PromptAttack).
- [Catastrophic Jailbreak of Open-source LLMs via Exploiting Generation](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/17706)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “Catastrophic Jailbreak of Open-source LLMs via Exploiting Generation” investigate?
  - **What they did:** Finally, the authors propose an effective alignment method that explores diverse generation strategies, which can reasonably reduce the attack success rate under their attack.
- [GPT-4 Is Too Smart To Be Safe: Stealthy Chat with LLMs via Cipher](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/18817)
  - **Research question:** What LLM or agent attack surface, failure mode, or defense does “GPT-4 Is Too Smart To Be Safe: Stealthy Chat with LLMs via Cipher” investigate?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a novel framework CipherChat to systematically examine the generalizability of safety alignment to non-natural languages -- ciphers.

## 2. Backdoors, Poisoning, and Adversarial ML

- [Towards Poisoning Fair Representations](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/18425)
  - **Research question:** How does “Towards Poisoning Fair Representations” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose the first data poisoning framework attacking FRL.
- [BaDExpert: Extracting Backdoor Functionality for Accurate Backdoor Input Detection](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/17666)
  - **Research question:** How does “BaDExpert: Extracting Backdoor Functionality for Accurate Backdoor Input Detection” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors present a novel defense, against backdoor attacks on Deep Neural Networks (DNNs), wherein adversaries covertly implant malicious behaviors (backdoors) into DNNs.
- [Certified Adversarial Robustness for Rate Encoded Spiking Neural Networks](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/19423)
  - **Research question:** How does “Certified Adversarial Robustness for Rate Encoded Spiking Neural Networks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce novel adversarial training algorithms for rate-encoded models that significantly improve the state-of-the-art empirical robust accuracy result.
- [Like Oil and Water: Group Robustness Methods and Poisoning Defenses May Be at Odds](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/17694)
  - **Research question:** How does “Like Oil and Water: Group Robustness Methods and Poisoning Defenses May Be at Odds” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Moreover, scrutinizing recent poisoning defenses both in centralized and federated learning, the authors observe that they rely on similar heuristics to identify which samples should be eliminated as poisons.
- [Universal Backdoor Attacks](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/19514)
  - **Research question:** How does “Universal Backdoor Attacks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors show this is not necessarily true and more efficient, universal data poisoning attacks exist that allow controlling misclassifications from any source class into any target class with a slight increase in poison samples.
- [Towards Reliable and Efficient Backdoor Trigger Inversion via Decoupling Benign Features](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/18542)
  - **Research question:** How does “Towards Reliable and Efficient Backdoor Trigger Inversion via Decoupling Benign Features” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors also exploit their BTI module to further design backdoor-removal and pre-processing-based defenses.
- [Adversarial Feature Map Pruning for Backdoor](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/18966)
  - **Research question:** How does “Adversarial Feature Map Pruning for Backdoor” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose Adversarial Feature Map Pruning for Backdoor (FMP) to mitigate backdoor from the DNN.
- [Backdoor Contrastive Learning via Bi-level Trigger Optimization](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/17799)
  - **Research question:** How does “Backdoor Contrastive Learning via Bi-level Trigger Optimization” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose a bi-level optimization approach to achieve this goal, where the inner optimization simulates the CL dynamics of a surrogate victim, and the outer optimization enforces the backdoor trigger to stay close to the target throughout the surrogate CL procedure.
- [Backdoor Secrets Unveiled: Identifying Backdoor Data with Optimized Scaled Prediction Consistency](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/19591)
  - **Research question:** How does “Backdoor Secrets Unveiled: Identifying Backdoor Data with Optimized Scaled Prediction Consistency” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** Subsequently, the authors develop a bi-level optimization-based approach to precisely identify backdoor data by minimizing the advanced SPC loss.
- [Bounding the Expected Robustness of Graph Neural Networks Subject to Node Feature Attacks](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/19134)
  - **Research question:** How does “Bounding the Expected Robustness of Graph Neural Networks Subject to Node Feature Attacks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors further introduce a probabilistic method to estimate the expected robustness, which allows us to evaluate the effectiveness of GCORN on several real-world datasets.
- [Sharpness-Aware Data Poisoning Attack](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/18316)
  - **Research question:** How does “Sharpness-Aware Data Poisoning Attack” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To address this challenge, the authors propose a new strategy called Sharpness-Aware Data Poisoning Attack (SAPA) .
- [Demystifying Poisoning Backdoor Attacks from a Statistical Perspective](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/19218)
  - **Research question:** How does “Demystifying Poisoning Backdoor Attacks from a Statistical Perspective” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors evaluate the effectiveness of any backdoor attack incorporating a constant trigger, by establishing tight lower and upper boundaries for the performance of the compromised model on both clean and backdoor test data.
- [BadChain: Backdoor Chain-of-Thought Prompting for Large Language Models](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/18305)
  - **Research question:** How does “BadChain: Backdoor Chain-of-Thought Prompting for Large Language Models” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors propose BadChain, the first backdoor attack against LLMs employing COT prompting, which does not require access to the training dataset or model parameters and imposes low computational overhead.
- [Boosting the Adversarial Robustness of Graph Neural Networks: An OOD Perspective](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/19149)
  - **Research question:** How does “Boosting the Adversarial Robustness of Graph Neural Networks: An OOD Perspective” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** To further evaluate adaptive robustness, the authors develop adaptive attacks against their methods, revealing a trade-off between graph attack efficacy and defensibility.

  - **Research question:** How does “Efficient Backdoor Attacks for Deep Neural Networks in Real-world Scenarios” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors introduce a more realistic attack scenario where victims collect data from multiple sources, and attackers cannot access the complete training data.
- [Adversarial Attacks on Fairness of Graph Neural Networks](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/17753)
  - **Research question:** How does “Adversarial Attacks on Fairness of Graph Neural Networks” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
  - **What they did:** The authors investigate the problem of adversarial attacks on fairness of GNNs and propose G-FairAttack, a general framework for attacking various types of fairness-aware GNNs in terms of fairness with an unnoticeable effect on prediction utility.

  - **Research question:** How does “Rethinking Backdoor Attacks on Dataset Distillation: A Kernel Method Perspective” expose, measure, or mitigate backdoor, poisoning, or adversarial risk in AI models?
